Good to know

How to spot a drain-unblocking scam

The Halle-Vilvoorde area is known for it. Four signs that should make you hang up:

  • A price that changes once the van is parked.
  • An invoice charged by the metre AND by the hour.
  • No address, no company number, no name.
  • Cash only, with no invoice.

We do the opposite. The price is given on the phone and confirmed at your door, the address and the company number are at the bottom of this page, and you always get an invoice.
