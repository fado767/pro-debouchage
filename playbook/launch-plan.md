*The one owner of the plan and its gates. Rewritten in place as steps close (a closed step is logged in `LOG.md` and its line here becomes "done, date"). Written 2026-08-22 at kickoff from the taxi business's two full client runs (`research/01`, section A) and today's research. The sequence is the thing: one step out of order costs a week.*

## 0. The critical chain, in one line

Address and name settled → domain in Roro's name → Workspace (info@ mailbox = Roro's Google login) → Business Profile created signed in as info@ → verification video filmed LIVE at the visit → (Google takes about 5 days) → page built and live on the domain meanwhile → Ads account created inside Roro's login with the live URL → campaign built, proven, enabled → reviews collected from the first jobs, added to the page as they come.

**Why this order.** The Business Profile video must be filmed inside the verification flow on a phone signed in as the OWNING account, so that account must exist before the visit. Google Ads refuses to finish an account whose destination URL does not resolve, so the page must be live before the Ads session. Verification takes about 5 days, so it starts first and everything else is built in its shadow.
