# playbook/ads-program.md, the campaign and its routine

*The one owner of how Google Ads is built and run for Pro Débouchage. Rewritten in place. Distilled 2026-08-22 from `research/03` part 2, the taxi ads program (`research/01` E) and the taxi Ads gotchas (`research/01` A5). Nothing here is a promise to Roro: no ROI number exists until week 8.*

## 1. Budget (DECIDED 2026-08-23 by Fady: start at 15 to 20 euro a day, use the 400-for-400 Google credit, raise on evidence; the arithmetic below is what we tell Roro when the first numbers are in)
Belgian plumber CPC benchmark: median about 12 euro, débouchage terms narrower, plan on 4 to 10 euro per click and correct in week one (`research/03` 2.1). 300 euro a month is 1 to 2 clicks a day, a lottery ticket. The research's number for a full test is 750 to 1,000 euro a month (25 to 33 euro a day, roughly 4 to 8 clicks a day, at a 10 percent call rate 12 to 24 calls a month at 40 to 85 euro per call), then decide on real numbers. Fady already told Roro 15 euro a day to start; 20 euro a day is the better opening because at 4 to 10 euro a click it buys 2 to 5 clicks a day instead of 1 to 3, and the 400 euro credit doubles the first 400 euro spent. Say the arithmetic, never the outcome; the raise to a real test budget comes when Roro has tasted the calls.

## 2. Structure, week one
- Search only. Search partners OFF, Display OFF, broad match OFF, AI Max and search-term matching and final-URL expansion OFF, automatically created assets OFF, auto-apply recommendations OFF.
- **Two campaigns, split by language** (budget control, true cost per call per language, each points at its own URL): about 50/50 at 15 to 20 euro a day total, corrected at week 2 on where the calls come from (the ring is Dutch-speaking on the north and Flemish side, French on the south).
- Ad groups, one intent each, four live at launch per language (urgent, WC, évier and canalisation, pompage cave), curage and fosse septique switched on in week three: FR-1 `/fr/debouchage-urgent`, FR-2 `/fr/wc-bouche`, FR-3 `/fr/canalisation-bouchee`, FR-6 `/fr/pompage-cave-inondee`; NL-1 `/nl/dringende-ontstopping`, NL-2 `/nl/verstopte-wc`, NL-3 `/nl/verstopte-afvoer`, NL-6 `/nl/kelder-leegpompen`.
- **RSAs plus call assets, not call-only ads** (Google retired new call-only ads in February 2026). One unpinned RSA per ad group at Ad strength Good (pinning forced Poor on the taxi accounts; message match is carried by the landing URL and the headline set, the taxi rule wins over the research's pin-to-position-1 advice unless strength stays Good). Call asset with call reporting ON and a Google forwarding number, scheduled as the real answering hours. Assets: business name, logo, four callouts, structured snippet (services), the LOCATION asset (needs the Business Profile manager link), sitelinks to the language variants.
- Geo: **listed towns, never a radius**, Location options = **Presence**, the RING around Brussels on both sides of the language border, **Brussels city communes EXCLUDED** (Roro, 2026-08-23: parking), list in `launch-plan.md` section 6. Language setting FR on the FR campaign, NL on the NL campaign. Keyword note: the `research/03` lists lean on "bruxelles"; keep a few "débouchage bruxelles" phrase terms because ring-town residents type it and the geo keeps the clicks in the ring, drop the Brussels commune keywords (schaerbeek, ixelles, anderlecht, ...), and add ring-town terms (wemmel, grimbergen, zaventem, dilbeek, halle, waterloo, wavre, nivelles, meise, asse, machelen, ...).
- Schedule: 24/7 only if Roro truly answers at night; then +15 to 25 percent bid for 22:00 to 06:00. If he does not, pause those hours: an unanswered emergency call is worse than no ad. Devices: mobile baseline, desktop minus 30 percent, tablet minus 50 percent, corrected after two weeks.
- Keywords: exact for money terms, phrase for discovery, about 25 exact plus 30 phrase per language (`research/03` 2.5 has the full lists). Negative list at account level from day one (`research/03` 2.5: gratuit, emploi, vacature, destop, comment déboucher, zelf, other provinces and cities, nez bouché, avis, arnaque, ...). Adding a negative is routine; removing one is a change.
- Bidding: weeks 1 and 2 Maximize Clicks with a CPC cap (6 euro FR, 5 euro NL); at 15 to 30 conversions in 30 days switch to Maximize Conversions; Target CPA only later, at about 20 percent above the observed cost per call. Never Maximize Conversions on day one.
- Performance Max: no in week one, revisit at month three. Local Services Ads: Belgium lists plumbers as eligible, eligibility at the postcode needs Google's own check and verification paperwork; a week-two task for Fady, not a week-one plan.

## 3. Ad copy
Six RSA headline sets per language are drafted in `research/03` 2.11 and 2.12 (30-character headlines, 90-character descriptions), sitelinks and callouts in 2.13. Rules: the phone number never in a HEADLINE (disapproved on the taxi account, fine in the call asset), no claim the page cannot back, the ad group's words in the headline and in the H1 of its URL.

## 4. Conversion tracking and the proof
- Actions in Roro's OWN Ads account (per-account tag `AW-...`, never cross-account): `call_click` (website phone click, primary, count one, value 1 euro flat, 30-day click window, enhanced conversions OFF), `calls from ads` (forwarding number, secondary, 45-second threshold), `whatsapp_click` (secondary, lower value). Secondary cannot be set at creation in the wizard: create, then Settings, Action optimization, Secondary. Remove any junk action the wizard auto-creates. Do it while the account has 0 campaigns.
- Consent Mode v2, ad_storage denied until consent; a refusing visitor fires nothing, an accepting one fires gtag for the AW id.
- **The tag proof is on the wire.** A pre-launch test conversion can never appear in the Ads UI. On the live domain, after accepting cookies, `performance.getEntriesByType('resource')` must show the gtag load and the `googleadservices.com/pagead/conversion/<id>/...label=...` beacon after a stubbed tel: click (WhatsApp stubbed so nothing reaches Roro). The first real post-launch conversion is the second proof.

## 6. The routine after launch
- **Hands off for two weeks** except negatives, a disapproval fix, and pausing a claim that stopped being true.
- **Daily, 10 minutes, first two weeks:** did it serve and did the budget last the day; search terms of the last 24 hours, add negatives; call clicks and reported calls matched against what Roro's phone did; the sticky call button still works on a real phone.
- **Weekly Monday check, 20 minutes:** pacing (spend divided by days live), search terms sorted good / junk / outside the area, conversions sanity (real traffic and zero conversions for two weeks means suspect the tag first), the 7-day snapshot (impressions, clicks, CTR, avg CPC, conversions, cost per conversion, search impression share as three columns: share, lost to budget, lost to rank), disapprovals, claims re-verified, one log line. Never bids, budget, copy, keywords, geo or strategy changes in the weekly check.
- **Checkpoints are the only moments settings change:** weeks 2, 4, 6 and 8 from the launch date. Week 2: negatives batch, first honest read, geo check. Week 4: one RSA edit per ad group on CTR evidence, one bid-cap notch with written reasoning, neighbouring towns added by name, possible strategy switch. Week 6 light, read-only if a switch happened at week 4. Week 8: judgment, the proof number, put to Fady and Roro, with its distortions named (clicks are not jobs, consent refusers are uncounted, thin samples get no verdict).
- **The weekly one-page report to Roro:** spent, clicks and avg CPC, call clicks plus reported calls plus WhatsApp clicks, cost per call, the five most expensive search terms and what we did, one sentence on next week, and the one question every week: which calls became paid jobs. Without that answer we measure noise. A daily one-minute Tally "jobs today" form for Roro's wife is the precise version of the same idea (build in week two).
- What each number triggers: near-zero impressions after week one is a mechanical fault, never fixed with budget; CTR under 2 percent is weak; zero conversions at 50 to 100 clicks means tag, then page, then keywords, in that order; impression share lost to budget points at budget, lost to rank at relevance and copy, never both at once.
