## 1. Budget (DECIDED 2026-08-23: start 15 to 20 euro a day, the 400-for-400 credit, raise on evidence)
Plan on 4 to 10 euro a click and correct in week one; débouchage-specific Belgian CPCs are unpublished, so a **Keyword Planner pull on the launch list is a pre-flight gate**: if FR exact terms come back above 7 to 8 euro, the budget conversation reopens with Fady and weight shifts to the NL terms (thinner auctions). 20 a day is the better opening (2 to 5 clicks a day instead of 1 to 3). The credit is claimed at the account session, and the qualifying spend must land inside its window (spend-and-match, roughly 60 days, credited about 35 days later, expires unspent). Billing reality to tell Roro: new accounts charge at a low threshold (about 50 euro), so frequent small card charges at first. Overdelivery is normal: up to 2x the daily budget on a day, capped at 30.4x monthly (about 547 euro at 18 a day). Say the arithmetic, never the outcome; the raise to a real test budget (25 to 33 a day) comes when Roro has tasted the calls.

## 2. Structure (DECIDED 2026-08-26, replaces the two-campaign plan)
- **Search only. ONE campaign, TWO ad groups: FR emergency and NL emergency.** One budget, one bidding strategy, shared learning. Grounds: at this budget a split starves both halves, and Google removes the campaign language setting from Search in late September 2026 anyway; the FR/NL separation lives in the keyword language, the ad language and the landing URL, which is exactly what survives. Language setting (while it exists): ALL languages, both ad groups cover the full town list; Belgians mix interface and query language, the keyword self-selects the user.
- **Switch-off checklist at creation, all default-ON traps: AI Max OFF (all three: search term matching, text customization, final URL expansion), Search partners OFF, Display expansion OFF, automatically created assets OFF.** After creation, account level: every auto-apply recommendation OFF, then the History tab checked for anything applied first. Google rep "optimization" calls: politely declined. Launching exact-match-only with ACA off also keeps us out of the forced AI Max migration (1 September 2026).
- **Keywords (DECIDED 2026-08-26: emergency-only at launch, the rest on evidence).** Per language roughly 8 to 15 keywords, exact plus phrase: tier 1 emergency (débouchage/déboucheur/ontstopping(sdienst) + town incl. - **Bidding (DECIDED 2026-08-26): Maximize Conversions from day one**, conversion tracking proven on the wire first (G5). Hands off 14 days except negatives (learning resets on budget changes above about 15 percent, target changes, structural edits). **Documented fallback:** if the campaign barely serves after 10 to 14 days, Maximize Clicks with CPC caps (6 euro FR terms, 5 euro NL) for two to three weeks purely to buy data, then back. Target CPA only at about 30 conversions in 30 days, realistically months away.
- **Schedule (DECIDED 2026-08-26): 24/7 flat, zero bid adjustments.** Schedule and device bid adjustments are IGNORED under Smart Bidding, so the old night and device modifiers are dead letters; night and weekend availability is said in the copy, and the algorithm finds the hours from call data. 24/7 answering is confirmed (business-brief section 1).

## 3. Ad copy (DECIDED 2026-08-26: promise yes, numbers no)
- **Lead with price certainty and anti-scam trust** (the market's open wound, research/24 section 3), speed second, real 24/7 third: "prix annoncé avant les travaux", "vous parlez à celui qui vient", the town in the headline, the call instruction in the headline. **No price NUMBERS in ads at launch** (VAT wording still moving on one service; numbers reconsidered at week 2 with the VAT settled and the claim page-backed).
- **No English ads at launch** (DECIDED 2026-08-26); the /en/ page keeps serving people who switch language. Revisit at week 8 only if search terms show real English queries.
- Standing rules: the phone number never in a HEADLINE (call asset only), no claim the page cannot back (every claim re-verified against the page before launch), the insurance report stays OUT of all ads ("sur demande" only), no em dashes, FR and NL each written to convert in their own voice, never translated. Draft RSA material: `research/14` (rework into the two-ad-group shape at the build, keeping the headline sets as message-match variants inside each group).

## 4. Conversion tracking and the proof
- Actions in Roro's OWN Ads account (per-account tag `AW-...`, never cross-account): `call_click` (website tel: click, primary, count one, value 1 euro flat, 30-day window, enhanced conversions OFF), `calls from ads` (Google forwarding number on the call asset, secondary, **60-second threshold**, raised from 45 per research/24: a real job discussion runs longer, a wrong number does not), `whatsapp_click` (secondary, lower value). Secondary is set after creation (Settings, Action optimization). Junk wizard actions removed. All while the account has 0 campaigns.
- **Website call snippet decision parked at week 2:** the phone snippet (swaps the displayed number for ad visitors, counts call duration) would upgrade call_click quality, but the number must stay text the snippet can find, and forwarding numbers are Google property (route about 60 days, NEVER on the van, cards or the Business Profile). Not a launch blocker.
- Consent Mode v2, ad_storage denied until consent; refusers fire nothing and are uncounted, the week-8 judgment names that distortion.
- **The tag proof is on the wire** (G5): on the live domain, after accepting cookies, `performance.getEntriesByType('resource')` shows the gtag load and the `googleadservices.com/pagead/conversion/<id>/...label=...` beacon after a stubbed tel: click. The first real post-launch conversion is the second proof.

## 6. The routine after launch
- **Hands off for two weeks** except negatives, a disapproval fix, and pausing a claim that stopped being true. The learning period is real: budget nudges above about 15 percent reset it.
- **Daily, 10 minutes, first two weeks:** did it serve and did the budget last the day; search terms of the last 24 hours, add negatives; call clicks and reported calls matched against what Roro's phone did; the sticky call button still works on a real phone.
- **Weekly Monday check, 20 minutes:** pacing, search terms sorted good / junk / outside the area, conversions sanity (real traffic and zero conversions for two weeks means suspect the tag first), the 7-day snapshot (impressions, clicks, CTR, avg CPC, conversions, cost per conversion, impression share with lost-to-budget and lost-to-rank), disapprovals, claims re-verified, one log line. Never bids, budget, copy, keywords, geo or strategy changes in the weekly check.
- **Checkpoints are the only moments settings change:** weeks 2, 4, 6 and 8. Week 2: negatives batch, first honest read, geo check, the landing-page variants decision (built by then, pointed on evidence), the phone-snippet decision, price-in-ads reconsidered if VAT settled. Week 4: one RSA edit per ad group on CTR evidence, service keywords (camera, septic, curage) considered on call data, neighbouring towns by name, fallback-mode bid-cap notch with written reasoning if in fallback. Week 6 light. Week 8: judgment, the proof number, put to Fady and Roro with its distortions named (clicks are not jobs, consent refusers uncounted, thin samples get no verdict). English ads reconsidered here at the earliest.
- **Rainy-week headroom:** autumn is the season peak and storm days spike the cheapest desperate calls of the year (cellar pumping); a temporary budget raise on a storm week is a named, logged exception, not a drift.
- **The weekly one-page report to Roro:** spent, clicks and avg CPC, call clicks plus reported calls plus WhatsApp clicks, cost per call, the five most expensive search terms and what we did, one sentence on next week, and the one question every week: which calls became paid jobs. The daily one-minute Tally "jobs today" form for Roro's wife is the precise version of the same idea (build in week two).
- What each number triggers: near-zero impressions after week one is a mechanical fault, never fixed with budget; CTR under 2 percent is weak; zero conversions at 50 to 100 clicks means tag, then page, then keywords, in that order; impression share lost to budget points at budget, lost to rank at relevance and copy, never both at once.
