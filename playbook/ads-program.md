## 1. Budget (DECIDED 2026-08-23: start 15 to 20 euro a day, the 400-for-400 credit, raise on evidence)
Plan on 4 to 10 euro a click and correct in week one; débouchage-specific Belgian CPCs are unpublished, so a **Keyword Planner pull on the launch list is a pre-flight gate**: if FR exact terms come back above 7 to 8 euro, the budget conversation reopens with Fady and weight shifts to the NL terms (thinner auctions). 20 a day is the better opening (2 to 5 clicks a day instead of 1 to 3). The credit is claimed at the account session, and the qualifying spend must land inside its window (spend-and-match, roughly 60 days, credited about 35 days later, expires unspent). Billing reality to tell Roro: a new account charges at a low threshold and Google raises that threshold ITSELF as the account keeps paying. Ours started at 100 EUR and Google AUTO-RAISED it to 200 EUR on 9 September 2026, right after a 100 EUR charge, so the next card charge is about 200 EUR. That is Google's normal ladder (50, 100, 200, 350, 500); it changes WHEN the card is charged, never how much is spent. Tell Roro before a 200 EUR charge lands on his card. Overdelivery is normal: up to 2x the daily budget on a day, capped at 30.4x monthly (about 608 euro at 20 a day). Say the arithmetic, never the outcome; the raise to a real test budget (25 to 33 a day) comes when Roro has tasted the calls.

## 6. The routine after launch
- **Hands off for two weeks** except negatives, a disapproval fix, and pausing a claim that stopped being true. The learning period is real: budget nudges above about 15 percent reset it.
- **Daily, 10 minutes, first two weeks:** did it serve and did the budget last the day; search terms of the last 24 hours, add negatives; call clicks and reported calls matched against what Roro's phone did; the sticky call button still works on a real phone.
- **Weekly Monday check, 20 minutes:** pacing, search terms sorted good / junk / outside the area, conversions sanity (real traffic and zero conversions for two weeks means suspect the tag first), the 7-day snapshot (impressions, clicks, CTR, avg CPC, conversions, cost per conversion, impression share with lost-to-budget and lost-to-rank), disapprovals, claims re-verified, one log line. Never bids, budget, copy, keywords, geo or strategy changes in the weekly check.
- **Checkpoints are the only moments settings change:** weeks 2, 4, 6 and 8. Week 2: negatives batch, first honest read, geo check, the phone-snippet decision, price-in-ads reconsidered if VAT settled. The landing-page variants are NOT a week-2 item any more: they are not built, and section 10 owns this now and makes per-intent variants a week-4 refinement, never a cause. Week 4: one RSA edit per ad group on CTR evidence, service keywords (camera, septic, curage) considered on call data, neighbouring towns by name, fallback-mode bid-cap notch with written reasoning if in fallback. Week 6 light. Week 8: judgment, the proof number, put to Fady and Roro with its distortions named (clicks are not jobs, consent refusers uncounted, thin samples get no verdict). English ads reconsidered here at the earliest.
- **Rainy-week headroom:** autumn is the season peak and storm days spike the cheapest desperate calls of the year (cellar pumping); a temporary budget raise on a storm week is a named, logged exception, not a drift.
- **The weekly report to Roro is a WhatsApp DRAFT PLUS a call by Fady, never e-mail (DECISIONS 2026-09-03).** Fady sends the short draft and phones him during or right after; the "how many calls did you get" question is asked BY PHONE every week. Contents: spent, clicks and avg CPC, call clicks plus reported calls plus WhatsApp clicks, cost per call, the five most expensive search terms and what we did, one sentence on next week, and the one question every week: which calls became paid jobs. The filter and the style (DECISIONS 2026-08-31): only conversions confirmed REAL go into a Roro report, unverified call_clicks stay out (Ads keeps counting them; the filter is on what we tell the client), and the report is a few highlights, never a list of every open item. The daily one-minute Tally "jobs today" form for Roro's wife is DROPPED (DECISIONS 2026-08-29): Roro prefers phoning Fady with the updates, so the weekly report feeds on those calls. The form stays parked and unused at tally.so/r/PdJkkB.
- What each number triggers: near-zero impressions after week one is a mechanical fault, never fixed with budget; CTR under 2 percent is weak; zero conversions at 50 to 100 clicks means tag, then page, then keywords, in that order; impression share lost to budget points at budget, lost to rank at relevance and copy, never both at once.

## 7. What "working" means (added 2026-08-30)
- **The success condition is a steady monthly flow of REAL JOBS**, month after month. Clicks, CTR, cost per click and impression share are diagnostics only: section 6 says what each of them triggers. A future session reading a good CTR and a quiet phone is reading a failure, not a success.
- **The ceiling is the client's own capacity, and he is willing to raise it.** Roro said on 2026-08-30 that if this goes well he may buy a SECOND VAN (`business-brief.md` section 8: he said it, he did not commit to it). So the second question next to jobs per month is always whether Roro is at capacity: leads he cannot serve are as much a signal as leads he does not get.
- **There is no target number and none is invented here.** No jobs-per-month figure, no cost-per-job figure and no date has been agreed with Roro or with Fady. Setting one waits for real data, and gate G6 stands: no ROI promise to Roro before the week-8 evidence exists.
