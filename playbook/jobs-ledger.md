# jobs-ledger.md, every call and job Roro reports

*The one owner of the jobs record (created 2026-09-18, DECISIONS that date). Fed by Roro's daily WhatsApp to Fady; Fady pastes it into a session, the session adds the rows. One row per call, the LOST ones included. No customer names, no phone numbers, no street addresses: town or postcode only. It is matched at every weekly Ads read against the Call details report and the daily click table (the day a call came in against the day's clicks), and it is the only place a cost per job may be computed from. Amounts are what Roro invoiced, VAT included. G6 still holds: this file reports what happened, it promises nothing.*
