# playbook/landing-page.md, the page spec

*The one owner of what the page is, what it says and how it is measured. Rewritten in place as decisions land. Distilled 2026-08-22 from `research/03` part 1 and `research/02` sections 3 and 4. Copy ships FR and NL together, simple words, no em dashes, nothing we cannot prove.*

## 1. The job of the page
A customer with water rising, phone in hand, calls within 8 seconds. Everything else serves that. Call first, WhatsApp second ("send a photo"), no form above the fold, no cookie wall, under 2 seconds on 4G.

## 2. What we may claim on day one (and what not)
- May: 24/7 including weekends and holidays (only if Roro really answers at night, see `ads-program.md` section 5), serving the ring around Brussels in Vlaams-Brabant and Brabant wallon (zone-first, never "based in Vilvoorde": section 4's VILVOORDE RULE) (named towns, never "toute la Belgique", and not Brussels city), camera inspection (free with the job, Roro's USP), high-pressure cleaning, sewer and sterput, septic emptying, flooded-cellar pumping, free quote, insurance report possible, the price announced before work starts (only if Roro commits to it), phone 0480 649 649.
- Not, until Roro confirms: "20 ans" (only as a named person's years in the trade), any satisfaction percentage, any review count, any arrival minutes number (only the number he can keep), a no-night-surcharge promise (only if true; if he adds a surcharge we print the amount), a guarantee and its duration, insurance and its insurer, payment methods.
- Never: invented staff, counters, stock testimonials, "toute la Belgique", a teaser price ("àpd 20 euro").

## 3. The angle (from the market)
Speed is table stakes (16 of 16 competitors say 24/7). **Price certainty is the differentiator**: the invoice that is not the phone price is the number one fear, Halle-Vilvoorde is a documented scam hotspot for this trade, and consumers are told to check the enterprise number, a written quote, the hourly price and a branded van. So the page shows, unasked: the enterprise number in the trust strip, a price table with "à partir de" floors Roro will honour, "le prix est annoncé avant de commencer", a named human (Afrim) and the van, real job photos with the commune, and FR plus NL (only 2 of 16 competitors are bilingual).

## 3b. Design direction (Fady, 2026-08-23, `DECISIONS.md`)
Copy first, then design. A BIG headline that shouts the pain point in Belgian French (the homeowner or tenant with the blocked drain recognises it, maybe smiles), a short scroll that explains everything, the call CTA always in reach. Structure like a landing-page expert would (offer, proof, objections, one action), clean modern UI, strong contrast, realistic imagery only (the real van, Afrim, real jobs, before-and-afters), no stock and no AI-looking pictures, well-designed review cards, a selfie-video review from a real customer if Roro gets one. Competitors (seen 2026-08-23): generic, stock and AI images, bad contrast, text-stuffed pages, some clearly one player's network; but they have profiles, reviews and a prominent CTA. We match those three and beat them on copy and design. The three candidates below are the starting point, not the ceiling: the design brief may propose sharper pain-point headlines for Fady's pick.

## 6. Mobile and legal musts
- Real tel: links everywhere, number also as text. Buttons 64 to 80 px, 4.5:1 contrast. One hero image WebP, lazy load below the fold, no third-party script before consent except nothing.
- Three real language pages `/fr/`, `/nl/` and `/en/` with a header switch "FR | NL | EN" and hreflang; no auto-detection (Brussels trap). Copy is native persuasive per language, never literal translation (AGENTS section 6).
- Belgian law (art. III.74 CDE): name, seat address, e-mail, enterprise number, VAT number on the page; consumer prices VAT included. Cookie banner per the APD checklist: "Refuser tout" as visible and easy as "Accepter tout", no pre-ticked boxes, scrolling is not consent, no cookie wall, Google tag in Consent Mode v2 with ad_storage denied until consent. Privacy and cookie pages FR and NL.
- Old site: the fake About page, the counters and the sample page come down (or the whole .com redirects) the day we go live.

## 7. Message match and variants
One template, generated outputs. The H1 repeats the ad group: FR `/fr/debouchage-urgent`, `/fr/wc-bouche`, `/fr/canalisation-bouchee`, `/fr/pompage-cave-inondee` (and later `/fr/curage-canalisation`, `/fr/vidange-fosse-septique`); NL `/nl/dringende-ontstopping`, `/nl/verstopte-wc`, `/nl/verstopte-afvoer`, `/nl/kelder-leegpompen` (later `/nl/riool-ruimen`, `/nl/septische-put-ledigen`). Minimum at launch: `/fr/` and `/nl/` with the urgent H1; target: the eight starting variants, since the generator makes them cheap. Phone, price promise and sticky bar identical on all.

## 8. How we measure
`call_click` on any tel: link (primary conversion, Ads tag fired directly, no Tag Manager), `calls from ads` via a Google forwarding number in the call asset (Belgium supported; threshold owned by `ads-program.md` section 4), `whatsapp_click` (secondary, lower value), `scroll_75` and `time_30s` as diagnostics in GA4 only. Prove the tag on the wire before launch (`ads-program.md` section 4). Weekly: impressions, clicks, cost, call clicks, reported calls, WhatsApp clicks, cost per call, top search terms, and Roro's answer to "which calls became paid jobs".
