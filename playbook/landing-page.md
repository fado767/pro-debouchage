# playbook/landing-page.md, the page spec

*The one owner of what the page is, what it says and how it is measured. Rewritten in place as decisions land. Distilled 2026-08-22 from `research/03` part 1 and `research/02` sections 3 and 4. Copy ships FR and NL together, simple words, no em dashes, nothing we cannot prove.*

## 1. The job of the page
A customer with water rising, phone in hand, calls within 8 seconds. Everything else serves that. Call first, WhatsApp second ("send a photo"), no form above the fold, no cookie wall, under 2 seconds on 4G.

## 2. What we may claim on day one (and what not)
- May: 24/7 including weekends and holidays (only if Roro really answers at night, see `ads-program.md` section 5), based in Vilvoorde, serving the ring around Brussels in Vlaams-Brabant and Brabant wallon (named towns, never "toute la Belgique", and not Brussels city), camera inspection (free with the job, Roro's USP), high-pressure cleaning, sewer and sterput, septic emptying, flooded-cellar pumping, free quote, insurance report possible, the price announced before work starts (only if Roro commits to it), phone 0480 649 649.
- Not, until Roro confirms: "20 ans" (only as a named person's years in the trade), any satisfaction percentage, any review count, any arrival minutes number (only the number he can keep), a no-night-surcharge promise (only if true; if he adds a surcharge we print the amount), a guarantee and its duration, insurance and its insurer, payment methods.
- Never: invented staff, counters, stock testimonials, "toute la Belgique", a teaser price ("àpd 20 euro").

## 3. The angle (from the market)
Speed is table stakes (16 of 16 competitors say 24/7). **Price certainty is the differentiator**: the invoice that is not the phone price is the number one fear, Halle-Vilvoorde is a documented scam hotspot for this trade, and consumers are told to check the enterprise number, a written quote, the hourly price and a branded van. So the page shows, unasked: the enterprise number in the trust strip, a price table with "à partir de" floors Roro will honour, "le prix est annoncé avant de commencer", a named human (Afram) and the van, real job photos with the commune, and FR plus NL (only 2 of 16 competitors are bilingual).

## 3b. Design direction (Fady, 2026-08-23, `DECISIONS.md`)
Copy first, then design. A BIG headline that shouts the pain point in Belgian French (the homeowner or tenant with the blocked drain recognises it, maybe smiles), a short scroll that explains everything, the call CTA always in reach. Structure like a landing-page expert would (offer, proof, objections, one action), clean modern UI, strong contrast, realistic imagery only (the real van, Afram, real jobs, before-and-afters), no stock and no AI-looking pictures, well-designed review cards, a selfie-video review from a real customer if Roro gets one. Competitors (seen 2026-08-23): generic, stock and AI images, bad contrast, text-stuffed pages, some clearly one player's network; but they have profiles, reviews and a prominent CTA. We match those three and beat them on copy and design. The three candidates below are the starting point, not the ceiling: the design brief may propose sharper pain-point headlines for Fady's pick.

## 5. Structure, one page, mobile first
S0 sticky call bar (full width, 64 px, real `tel:+32480649649`, "Appeler 0480 649 649" / "Bel 0480 649 649", small WhatsApp button beside it, nothing may cover it) · S1 hero: H1, sub, big call button, one real photo (van or Afram at work), no form · S2 trust strip, three honest items: "24h/24, 7j/7", "Basé à Vilvoorde, autour de Bruxelles", "Prix annoncé avant de commencer" (swap for "Entreprise enregistrée, BCE 0xxx" once confirmed) · S3 six service cards each with its own call link: débouchage urgent, WC et évier, curage haute pression, inspection caméra (gratuite), vidange fosse septique, pompage cave inondée · S4 how it works in four steps (vous appelez, on annonce un prix, on vient avec le matériel, on confirme sur place et on débouche) · S5 prices, honestly: table with "à partir de" per job, VAT treatment stated (consumer prices VAT included by law), the déplacement rule, the night and weekend rule as it truly is · S6 what we bring: camera, jetting machine, pump, the van, one photo each (the honest substitute for reviews) · S7 zone: Vilvoorde first, then the ring communes on both sides of the language border, Brussels city excluded (`launch-plan.md` section 6) · S8 reviews, day-one version: one honest line "Nous démarrons notre page Google. Après l'intervention, nous vous demanderons un avis honnête." and the Google widget once 3 real reviews exist · S9 FAQ, eight questions (cost, night surcharge, déplacement, how fast, do you break things, insurance report, which communes, how to pay), FAQPage JSON-LD · S10 final call block with the number written out, hours, address · S11 footer: legal name, seat address, enterprise number, VAT number, e-mail, phone, privacy and cookie policy links.

## 6. Mobile and legal musts
- Real tel: links everywhere, number also as text. Buttons 64 to 80 px, 4.5:1 contrast. One hero image WebP, lazy load below the fold, no third-party script before consent except nothing.
- Two real language pages `/fr/` and `/nl/` with a header switch "FR | NL" and hreflang; no auto-detection (Brussels trap).
- Belgian law (art. III.74 CDE): name, seat address, e-mail, enterprise number, VAT number on the page; consumer prices VAT included. Cookie banner per the APD checklist: "Refuser tout" as visible and easy as "Accepter tout", no pre-ticked boxes, scrolling is not consent, no cookie wall, Google tag in Consent Mode v2 with ad_storage denied until consent. Privacy and cookie pages FR and NL.
- Old site: the fake About page, the counters and the sample page come down (or the whole .com redirects) the day we go live.

## 7. Message match and variants
One template, generated outputs. The H1 repeats the ad group: FR `/fr/debouchage-urgent`, `/fr/wc-bouche`, `/fr/canalisation-bouchee`, `/fr/pompage-cave-inondee` (and later `/fr/curage-canalisation`, `/fr/vidange-fosse-septique`); NL `/nl/dringende-ontstopping`, `/nl/verstopte-wc`, `/nl/verstopte-afvoer`, `/nl/kelder-leegpompen` (later `/nl/riool-ruimen`, `/nl/septische-put-ledigen`). Minimum at launch: `/fr/` and `/nl/` with the urgent H1; target: the eight starting variants, since the generator makes them cheap. Phone, price promise and sticky bar identical on all.

## 8. How we measure
`call_click` on any tel: link (primary conversion, Ads tag fired directly, no Tag Manager), `calls from ads` via a Google forwarding number in the call asset (Belgium supported; 45-second threshold), `whatsapp_click` (secondary, lower value), `scroll_75` and `time_30s` as diagnostics in GA4 only. Prove the tag on the wire before launch (`ads-program.md` section 4). Weekly: impressions, clicks, cost, call clicks, reported calls, WhatsApp clicks, cost per call, top search terms, and Roro's answer to "which calls became paid jobs".
