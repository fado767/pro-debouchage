### Ranked top three

1. **#1 (pain).** Three symptoms in the customer's own words, then a promise of relief in five words. It is the only candidate a person recognises before they finish reading, and the sub-line still carries the decided price-certainty angle. Best H1 for `/fr/` and `/nl/` generic.
2. **#4 (price certainty).** The decided differentiator said as plainly as it can be said, and it names the exact fear (the number changing once the van is parked). Best H1 for the `debouchage-urgent` variant where the visitor already knows what they need.
3. **#8 (humour-light).** The only one that earns a smile, and it flatters the customer instead of scaring them. Worth testing as the brand-recall version once the first two have data. Not the launch H1.

## 4. Design system

**Palette, derived from the logo** (the logo interlocks a `d` and a `p` in a blue to teal to green gradient against a yellow to orange to red gradient, with the wordmark PRO in blue and DEBOUCHAGE in orange). The logo carries six colours; the page deliberately carries three plus neutrals.

| Token | Hex | Role | Contrast check |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--ink` | `#102A4A` | primary, deep navy from the logo's blue end. Headlines, header, footer, dark sections. | white on ink 14.5:1, AAA |
| `--cta` | `#D63A17` | THE call button, and nothing else. Darkened from the site's live `#FF613C` so white text passes. | white on cta 4.69:1, AA |
| `--cta-hover` | `#B72F11` | pressed and hover state. | white 6.4:1 |
| `--accent` | `#0B7A70` | the camera and price-certainty badges, ticks, the WhatsApp secondary button outline. | white on accent 5.2:1, AA |
| `--flag` | `#FFD635` | the logo yellow, used only as a 4 px underline or a small highlight, never behind white text. | ink on flag 10.3:1 |
| `--paper` | `#F7F5F2` | page background, warm not grey. | |
| `--card` | `#FFFFFF` | cards, table. | ink on white 15.9:1 |
| `--line` | `#E2DED8` | hairlines only, never for text. | |
| `--body` | `#1B2733` | body text on paper. | 13.8:1, AAA |
| `--muted` | `#5A6672` | captions, legal lines. | 5.6:1, AA |

`#FF613C` (the live site's accent) is kept **only** for display-size type and large graphic shapes, where 3:1 is enough. It never carries small white text.

**Type.** Google Fonts, both with complete French and Dutch diacritics (é è ê ë à â ç î ï ô û ù ü ij, and the œ ligature).
- Display: **Archivo** at 800, condensed tracking `-0.02em`. It is already the font the current site loads, so it is continuity rather than a new brand decision. H1 clamp `2.25rem` to `4rem`, line-height 1.05.
- Body and UI: **Inter** at 400 and 600, `1.0625rem` on mobile, line-height 1.6, measure capped at 62 characters.
- Numbers (phone, prices): Inter with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. The phone number is set at display size and is always real text, never an image.

**Spacing rhythm.** 4 px base, scale 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 96. Section padding 48 px mobile, 96 px desktop. Card radius 16 px, button radius 12 px, image radius 20 px. Alternate `--paper` and `--card` section backgrounds so the short scroll still reads as chapters, with one `--ink` section (S6) as the visual anchor.

**Buttons.**
- Primary call: `--cta`, white 700 text, 64 px tall mobile / 56 px desktop, full width minus 16 px gutters, radius 12 px, no gradient, shadow `0 2px 0 var(--cta-hover)` so it reads as a physical key. Phone icon on the left, the number inside the label.
- Secondary WhatsApp: outlined in `--accent`, 48 px, at most 60 percent of the primary's width, labelled `Envoyer une photo par WhatsApp` / `Stuur een foto via WhatsApp`. Never the same size or colour as the call button.
- Tertiary text links in `--ink` with a `--flag` underline on hover.
- Focus ring: 3 px `--accent` at 2 px offset, visible on every interactive element.

**Sticky call bar (mobile).** Fixed bottom, 64 px plus `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, `--cta` background, full-width tap target, `tel:+32480649649`, a 44 px WhatsApp square on the right at 25 percent width. `z-index` above everything except the cookie banner, and the banner is styled so it sits ABOVE the bar and never covers it. `body { padding-bottom: 80px }` so the footer is never hidden. On desktop the bar becomes a sticky header button.

**Review card.** White card, radius 16 px, 24 px padding, hairline `--line`, no shadow. Top row: 40 px round avatar (the Google initial, never a stock face), the name as Google gives it, and the date in `--muted`. Then five stars in `--flag` at 18 px. Then the review text at 1rem, three lines clamped with a `Lire la suite` / `Lees meer` toggle. Bottom row: a small Google mark plus `Avis Google` / `Google-beoordeling`, linked to the profile. Cards scroll horizontally on mobile with snap points, three across on desktop. **Today the grid holds one full-width honest card instead** (S8 copy), styled identically so the day reviews land nothing needs redesigning. One card slot is built at 9:16 to hold a portrait selfie video, muted, poster frame, play on tap, captions burned in, so a real customer video drops straight in.

**Before-and-after component.** Two square photos side by side on desktop, stacked on mobile with a `--flag` divider, labels `Avant` / `Après` and `Voor` / `Na` in small caps, and one caption line under both: job type, commune, month, for example `Sterput bouché, Grimbergen, août 2026`. No slider handle: it hides one half on mobile and adds JavaScript for nothing. Both images same crop, same distance, same orientation.

**Imagery rule.** Real photos only, shot on a phone in daylight, lightly colour-corrected and never composited. No stock, no AI generation, no smiling models. If a photo does not exist, the slot ships as a plain colour block with a caption, not a stand-in image.

## 5. What must NOT be on the page

- No invented staff, no counters, no stock testimonials, no review count, no satisfaction percentage. The current site's About page and its counters are exactly what we are replacing.
- No "plus de 20 ans" for the company. Only ever as a named person's years in the trade, and only once Roro confirms whose years and in what.
- No arrival-minutes number until Roro gives one he can keep. No guarantee, no duration, no insurer name until confirmed.
- No "pas de majoration la nuit" unless it is true. If there is a surcharge, the amount is printed.
- No teaser price ("àpd 20 euro"), no "prix les plus bas", no price shown excluding VAT to a consumer.
- No "toute la Belgique", no "à Bruxelles" / "in Brussel" as a served place, no radius language.
- No legal name, BCE or VAT number until the HB Débouchage question is settled. No address on the Business Profile before that gate.
- No form above the fold, no online booking, no chat widget, no cookie wall, no pre-ticked consent, no tag firing before consent, nothing overlapping the sticky call bar.
- No AI-generated or stock imagery, no phone number rendered as an image, no auto-detection of language.
