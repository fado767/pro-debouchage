## 0. The headline findings, one screen

1. **Google changed the platform under our plan.** Three changes since research/14 was written: AI Max is ON by default in new Search campaigns and force-migrates broad-match/auto-asset campaigns from 1 September 2026; the campaign-level LANGUAGE setting is removed from Search campaigns in late September 2026; call-only ads can no longer be created (since February 2026), calls now go through RSAs with call assets. And under Smart Bidding, ad-schedule and device BID ADJUSTMENTS are ignored, which kills our planned night and device modifiers as written.
2. **The two-campaign FR/NL split is challenged from two directions.** The fundamentals research says at 15 to 20 euro/day do not split (each half starves below the learning floor; Google removes the language lever anyway): ONE campaign, TWO ad groups (FR, NL). The keyword research independently says set language targeting to ALL (Belgians mix interface language and query language) and let keyword language select the user. Recommendation below, Fady decides at the ads session.
3. **The niche playbook exists and we mostly already follow it.** A dozen independent home-services PPC specialists converge: emergency intent first, exact and phrase only, negatives from hour one, the call is the only conversion, ads only when the phone is answered. The sharpest point: at this budget, spend everything on emergency debouchage/ontstopping intent and let camera, septic and curage wait for call data. Never touch the "plombier" auction (Belgian median 11.94 euro CPC, spikes past 30).
4. **The Belgian market's open wound is trust, and our locked page is already the answer.** Test-Achats documents bait-price scams (59 euro ads, 800 euro invoices); most competitors anchor prices excluding VAT with add-on menus; almost nobody serves both languages; nobody shows a face or a real address. Our page (all-in TVAC prices, named human, enterprise number, no fake claims) is the exact counter-position. The ads should lead with price certainty, not speed.
5. **The keyword map is built and Belgian-authentic** (verified on Belgian sites and forums, not translated): FR "deboucheur" as a category word, "chambre de visite", "sterput"; Flemish "ontstoppingsdienst", "spoedontstopping", "ruimdienst". Full tiers and negative lists in section 4, ready to merge into the build package.

## 1. Platform facts, current as of 2026-08-26 (fundamentals agent, Opus)

**Deadlines and removals:**
- Since 3 Aug 2026: campaign-level broad match and legacy Automatically Created Assets can no longer be created. From 1 Sep 2026: campaigns using either are auto-migrated to AI Max, no opt-out. A campaign launched exact-match-only with ACA off is NOT eligible for forced migration. That is the protection.
- New Search campaigns have AI Max ON by default. Three switches under Campaign Settings, AI Max: search term matching, text customization, final URL expansion. Turn all three OFF. Practitioner data is damning for small local accounts: HBT Digital home-services test, 964 of 993 AI Max terms got zero clicks; Monks, 99 percent of ~30,000 AI Max terms zero conversions; SMEC 250+ campaigns, median CPA +16 percent; ~84 percent of advertisers report neutral or negative. Google claims +7 to +14 percent. Trust the practitioners at our budget.
- Late September 2026: the language setting disappears from Search campaigns. Eligibility will be read from ad creative language, landing page language and Google's read of the user. Our /fr/ and /nl/ URLs become an actual targeting input. Good news: we have separate URLs per language already.
- February 2026: call-only ads dead (existing ones stop Feb 2027). Route to calls: RSAs with CALL ASSETS. Google decides when to show the number, so urgency and the call instruction must be IN the headlines ("Bel nu", "Appelez, on s'en occupe" style), which also signals call intent to Google.
- Bid adjustments under Smart Bidding: ad-schedule percentage adjustments are IGNORED (the schedule hours are respected, the percentages are not). Under Maximize Conversions all bid adjustments are ignored except a -100 percent device exclusion. Consequence: the ads-program night modifier (+15 to 25 percent) and device modifiers (-30 desktop, -50 tablet) are dead letters. Run 24/7, no adjustments, put night and weekend availability into the COPY and let the algorithm find the hours.

**Bidding for a zero-history account, the honest disagreement:**
- Camp A (Jyll Saskin Gales, ex-Google, most-cited independent educator): Maximize Conversions from day one, because Smart Bidding uses auction-time context, not only history. Prerequisites: tracking live BEFORE spend, tight high-intent keywords.
- Camp B: Maximize Clicks with a strict CPC cap as a data-gathering phase.
- Merged recommendation for us: launch on Maximize Conversions, exact match, tight geo, tracking proven first. If after 10 to 14 days the campaign barely serves, widen slightly, and only then fall back to Max Clicks capped for two to three weeks. Target CPA not for months: the 30-conversions-in-30-days rule, which at our volume (~10 to 20 calls/month realistic) will not be met soon. Learning period 1 to 2 weeks; budget changes above ~15 to 20 percent reset it; the most likely self-inflicted wound at this budget is fiddling. Set it, leave it 14 days, negatives only.
- Budget reality stated plainly: practitioner floors are ~10 clicks/day; 15 to 20 euro/day buys maybe 3 to 6 clicks/day here. Workable ONLY as extreme concentration: fewest keywords, exact match, tight geo, one campaign. Keyword Planner CPC check before committing: if exact-match CPCs come back above 7 to 8 euro, the budget conversation reopens with Fady.

**Switch-off checklist at creation:** AI Max (all three), Search Partners, Display expansion, automatically created assets. After creation, account level: every auto-apply recommendation OFF (then check the History tab for anything applied first), decline Google rep "optimizations". PMax: no (published lead-gen data: Search beats PMax by 25 to 45 percent on cost per qualified lead; PMax cannot opt out of search partners; budget floors far above ours). SKAGs are dead; STAGs (single-theme ad groups) are the standard; merge any ad group under ~1,000 impressions/week.

**Call conversion mechanics (Belgium confirmed for Google forwarding numbers):** two actions, both wanted: calls from call assets (forwarding number, duration threshold) and calls to the site number via the phone SNIPPET that swaps the displayed number for ad visitors. Minimum call duration recommendation for this trade: 60 seconds. Warnings: the number in HTML must be text the snippet can find (not an image or hardcoded oddly), verify with Tag Assistant; forwarding numbers are Google property, route only ~60 days, NEVER on the van, cards or the Business Profile. Low-volume tactic available if duration-qualified conversions are near zero after three weeks: temporarily optimize on call clicks, then switch (accepting a learning reset).

**New-account gotchas:** advertiser identity verification can demand documents on short deadlines (as short as 48 hours reported; review up to 5, rarely 30, business days; miss it and the account pauses). Have ready BEFORE launch: KBO extract, enterprise number BE 1027.454.187, Roro's ID. False info during verification = Circumventing Systems = suspension. EU political-ads declaration is mandatory (we declare no). Billing: new accounts charge at a low threshold (~50 euro equivalent), so frequent small card charges at first, rising as history builds; warn the cardholder. Overdelivery: up to 2x daily budget on a day, capped at 30.4x monthly (~547 euro at 18/day). Intro credit: spend-and-match (~400 for 400 within ~60 days, credited ~35 days later, expires ~60 days after), one per advertiser tracked by domain and payment details; plan the qualifying spend inside the window.

**Suspension landscape:** 37 percent Circumventing Systems, 28 percent Unacceptable Business Practices (the emergency-callout bait-and-switch category, IMMEDIATE suspension, no warning). Google restricts locksmith ads in Belgium and banned plumber ads in the Netherlands outright (Feb 2024) over rogue emergency plumbers: this vertical is watched in Benelux. Protection = exactly our rules: visible business details, transparent prices with VAT stated, no unproven claims, page matches ads matches business. If anything goes wrong: appeal, never a second account.

## 2. The niche playbook (Fable agent)

Convergent consensus across LeadShutter, MWolf, ClicksGeek, Nomadclick (FR) and others:
- Emergency and planned work are two different businesses: emergency (wc bouche now, cellar under water) gets top bids, speed-and-availability copy, call formats; planned (camera, septic, maintenance) is price-shopped, waits at small budgets.
- Exact + phrase only until real conversion history. Negatives are the single highest-ROI action (20 to 40 percent waste cut); mine search terms weekly, expect 20 to 40 new negatives/week early.
- The conversion is the ANSWERED call. Emergency callers hitting voicemail dial the next result in under a minute. Ads run only when the phone is answered (ours: 24/7 confirmed, Roro 99 percent, wife for NL).
- Landing page: never a homepage, headline mirrors the search, giant tappable number, mobile-first (70 to 85 percent of these searches are on phones, often from the room with the problem). Service-specific pages convert 8 to 12 percent vs 2 to 4 for a homepage: the case for our per-ad-group variants.
- Economics: Belgian "plombier" median 11.94 euro CPC (Nexuro 2026, 264 keywords), emergency spikes past 30. Debouchage-specific Belgian CPCs are UNPUBLISHED: Keyword Planner check is mandatory pre-launch. France proxy: trade CPCs 5 to 10 euro, long-tail geo terms 30 to 50 percent cheaper. The whole strategy is forcing the cheap scenario: at 4 euro CPC and 15 percent click-to-call a call costs ~27 euro (fine); at 12 euro and 5 percent it costs 240 euro (dead). Plausible but unproven: Dutch ring-town auctions are thinner than FR Brussels ones; if Planner confirms FR terms above 7 to 8 euro, weight NL.
- Why small budgets fail: spread too thin, 1 to 2 mixed clicks/day, no learnable data, owner quits month two. 15 to 20 euro/day works only as a concentrated emergency-only account, not a miniature big account.
- Copy that converts mid-emergency (mature UK drain market): speed, fixed price up front, no call-out fee, real 24/7, backed by guarantee. Generic "besoin d'un plombier?" copy is the documented loser.
- Belgium specific: the rogue-operator wave (RTBF, Test-Achats: 59 euro ads becoming 800 euro invoices) makes honest price transparency a conversion WEAPON here, not just ethics. Autumn is peak season (leaves + rain), storm days spike cellar pumping: keep budget headroom for rainy October weeks, the cheapest desperate-customer calls of the year.
- Its 10 rules, condensed: 1 emergency intent only at first, two language slices, 8 to 15 keywords each, no plombier terms. 2 ads only when answered. 3 Presence-only geo, towns in the copy. 4 negatives day one, mined weekly. 5 calls the only KPI, judged on cost per answered call. 6 lead with the anti-scam promise. 7 problem + town headlines, never generic. 8 page mirrors ad. 9 no tCPA/broad/PMax until ~30 tracked calls. 10 verify CPCs in Planner and let them set expectations.

## 3. The Belgian competitive landscape (Opus agent)

Method: Google Ads Transparency Center (worked) + Bing ad slots (Google SERP bot-checked, not bypassed); page weights measured on fast desktop, mobile 4G is worse.

**Who buys clicks.** Dutch side is the bigger paid market: ONTSTOPPINGSSERVICE (~200 creatives), VDVK (~82), rioolprobleem (~56), Sobry (~55), Ontstoppingsdienst IG (~24), Rioolteam T&G (~15). French side thinner and fragmented: HBA deboucheur (~27), B.T.P DEBOUCHAGE (~28), LE DEBOUCHEUR LIEGEOIS (~18), plus small ones. **hb-debouchage.be is a confirmed Google advertiser** (the firm sharing our client's Vilvoorde mailbox address; young, incorporated 2024-09, FR+EN only, NO Dutch, excl-VAT from-prices from 90 euro, zero-call-out-fee hook, 5.0 stars on 18 reviews, published address 90 km from its registered one). **Customer price anchor (what a Belgian caller expects):** standard daytime unblocking ALL-IN 140 to 200 euro; toilet 120 to 200; sink/shower 90 to 175; sewer/main 200 to 400; camera 100 to 180 (up to 241); septic ~120 euro/m3; call-out fee 20 to 60 where charged; surcharges +50 evening/Saturday and +100 night/Sunday near-universal. **Structural weaknesses to exploit (their five, aligned with ours):** 1 nobody serves both languages properly (only Goossens has a real switcher, and it publishes nothing); 2 prices missing or weaponised (excl-VAT anchoring + add-on menus, worse than silence); 3 unverifiable and self-contradictory claims everywhere; 4 everyone anonymous, no face, no van, no recognisable address; 5 template networks pretend to serve 40 towns from 75 km away, a real local crew can make a keepable promise. A plain sentence explaining the rule plus all-in prices is unique in the vertical. (Our page already prices TVAC 6 percent; the curage/nettoyage VAT question with the accountant stays open, NOW.md.)

## 5. What this changes vs research/14 and ads-program.md (the synthesis)

Recommendations for the ads session, none of them decided until Fady says so:

1. **Campaign shape: ONE Search campaign, TWO ad groups (FR emergency, NL emergency), instead of two campaigns x four ad groups.** Grounds: budget floor arithmetic, shared learning data, the September language-setting removal, and the STAG consensus (merge anything under ~1,000 impressions/week, which four groups per language would trip constantly). Language separation lives in keyword language, ad language and landing URL (/fr/ vs /nl/), which is exactly what survives September. The research/14 ad-group copy is kept as headline material inside the two groups.
2. **Launch keyword set: lean emergency-only.** Tier 1 both languages plus the call-ready Tier 3 subset plus cave inondee (storm-driven, call-ready). Camera, septic, curage keywords wait for call data (was week 3 in the old plan; now: on evidence, not on a date). No plombier head terms ever at this budget.
3. **Bidding: Maximize Conversions from day one** (was: Max Clicks with 6/5 euro caps), with the documented fallback: if serving stalls after 10 to 14 days, Max Clicks capped for two to three weeks, then back. tCPA only at ~30 conversions/30 days, months away. Learning period discipline: 14 days hands off except negatives.
4. **Schedule: 24/7 flat, no bid adjustments at all** (they are ignored under Smart Bidding). Night and weekend availability goes in the copy. The old night modifier and device modifier lines in ads-program are obsolete as written.
5. **Switch-off list grows: AI Max all three components OFF** (new, critical, default-ON), plus the known Search Partners, Display, auto-apply, ACA.
6. **Copy leads with price certainty and anti-scam trust** (all-in TVAC prices on the page, price named before work, named human, enterprise number), speed second, real 24/7 third. Urgency and the call instruction in headlines because call assets show at Google's discretion. Never a price in ad copy until the VAT wording is fully settled and the claim is page-backed (unchanged rule); the "surcharges below the market's +100 convention" angle needs a research/17-style proof file before any use.
7. **Conversion actions: add the website phone SNIPPET decision.** research/14's call_click (tel: click) primary stands, but the fundamentals research recommends duration-qualified calls (60 s threshold) via forwarding numbers as the quality signal, with the documented low-volume fallback. The exact primary/secondary shape is an ads-session decision. Forwarding numbers never go on the van, cards or profile.
8. **Answers proposed to research/14's seven open questions:** 1 English ads: NO at launch, revisit after week 8 (budget concentration). 2 Insurance-report claim: stays out of ads (sur demande only). 3 Camera ad group: not at launch, on evidence. 4 Prices in ads: no at launch (VAT wording, plus claim-backing rule). 5 Schedule: 24/7 flat, modifiers dead. 6 Brussels-commune negatives: NOT needed with Presence-only targeting; keep the few "debouchage bruxelles" phrase terms (ring residents type them); do not negative the communes (it risks clipping those queries); watch search terms weekly instead. 7 Landing URLs: launch on /fr/ and /nl/ with headline message match, build the per-group variants in week two (the generator makes them cheap); do NOT hold launch for them.
9. **Pre-launch additions to the checklist:** Keyword Planner CPC pull for the launch list (if FR exact terms above 7 to 8 euro, budget conversation reopens, and weight shifts NL); verification pack ready (KBO extract, BE 1027.454.187, Roro's ID); warn the cardholder about frequent small threshold charges; plan the 400-for-400 qualifying spend inside its window; EU political-ads declaration (no).
10. **Positioning spine for all copy: "we are the ones coming, here is his name" vs the brokers, and the all-in price vs the excl-VAT add-on menus.** The competitor to beat on the NL side is Ontstoppingsdienst PRO (fast page, real prices, 45-min promise); the proof they cannot copy is Afrem's face, the van and a keepable local promise. Sobry's 337 reviews show what the review program is worth over time.
11. **Autumn timing note:** the season peak (leaves + rain) starts weeks after our launch window. Storm days are budget-headroom days, the cheapest desperate calls of the year. A rainy-week playbook line belongs in the ads routine.

**Facts that must be re-verified at the ads session (moving targets):** AI Max defaults and the migration scope, the language-setting removal date, LSA-into-PMax migration status for Belgium (week-2 LSA check stands, it needs the verified Business Profile anyway), current intro-credit terms for Belgium, forwarding-number availability unchanged, Keyword Planner CPCs.
