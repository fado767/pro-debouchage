# 16 - Prices for the three thin services, and the guarantee practice of the market

*Research, 2026-08-24. Fady's agency for Pro Débouchage. Fills the three gaps left by `research/07` (curage, fosse septique, pompage de cave) and adds the guarantee question. Zone: about 40 km around Wemmel, both sides of the language border, Brussels city excluded, so Brussels and Flemish sources are both in scope as market anchors. All figures fetched on 2026-08-24 unless the row says otherwise. Nothing here is a Pro Débouchage price. Every "PROPOSED" figure is Fady's proposal and needs Roro's yes.*

Resulting card ladder, consistent with `research/07`: 119 (évier) / 129 (WC) / 199 (égout with camera) / 229 (cave inondée) / 249 (curage 25 m) / 279 (fosse septique 3 m3).

**Three things that changed since `research/02` and `research/07` and that Roro or Fady must settle:**

1. **`research/02` said nobody publishes a cellar pumping price. That is now only partly true.** Three Belgian firms publish one (section 3). Nobody in our own zone does, so the gap is still ours to take, but the claim "nobody publishes it" must not be written as an absolute anywhere.
2. **VAT on a septic tank emptying is probably 21%, not 6%.** DrainUp states the reduced 6% rate applies to a pumping only when it precedes necessary building work in a home in use over 10 years. `research/07` assumed 6% across the board. This changes the fosse septique card and needs the accountant.
3. **In our own fire zone a cellar flooded by a natural flood is pumped free by the brandweer.** Zone Vlaams-Brabant West says so on its own retribution page. A paid cellar card therefore sells for burst pipes, groundwater and sewer backflow, not for storm floods, and saying that out loud is a trust win nobody else makes.

#### 1.2 The range and the average

Converting the HTVA figures at 6% to make them comparable, and taking the midpoint of each published band:

- **Observed range: about 120 to 450 EUR TVAC-comparable** for a curage of a house sewer line. Truck-scale industrial jobs run to 600 and beyond and nobody prices those online.
- **Median: about 225 EUR.**
- **Average: about 248 EUR.**

Two shapes exist in the market and they must not be confused. (a) Clearing a blocked sewer with the jetter, which is card 3 in `research/07` at a proposed 199. (b) A planned curage of a clean-ish line over 20 to 30 m, which is a longer job. M&S prices the two almost the same (haute pression 350, curage général 380), Plombier Urgence prices the curage by the metre band (150 to 300 for 20 to 30 m).

#### 1.3 PROPOSED price for Pro Débouchage

**Curage complet / volledige reiniging: 249 EUR TVAC, up to 25 m of line, first hour included, jetting machine included, camera check of the cleaned line included.**

Reasoning. It sits just above the 225 median and just above the 248 average, which is exactly the "reasonable, not cheap" position `research/07` chose for the other cards. It stays well under the M&S curage général (380 HTVA, so about 403 at 6%) and under the Plombier Wallonie truck band. It is coherent with card 3 at 199: the customer is told plainly that 199 clears the blockage and 249 cleans the whole line end to end, which is how M&S and Plombier Urgence justify the same gap. A defensible band if Roro wants to move it is **229 to 269 TVAC**; below 229 it starts to undercut card 3 and stops making sense.

#### 2.3 PROPOSED price for Pro Débouchage

- **Vidange fosse septique jusqu'à 3 m3: 279 EUR TVAC**, including the pumping, the legal disposal at an approved centre, and **the emptying certificate** (ruimingsattest in Flanders, attestation de vidange in Wallonia and Brussels).

The extra-m3 rate at 45 sits mid-market (30 to 65) and the distance rule copies Solvari, which is the clearest published version of it. **Including the certificate in the headline price is the differentiator**: the notary asks for it at a house sale and municipalities can check it, so it converts, and only Q Clean and DrainUp mention it at all.

**The VAT flag, and it is serious.** Every other card in `research/07` was computed at 6%. DrainUp says plainly that a septic pumping is **21% by default** and drops to 6% only when the pumping precedes necessary building work in a home in use for more than 10 years. Aquavlot also prices HTVA at 21%. If 21% applies, 279 TVAC is 230.58 HTVA. Roro's accountant must confirm which rate he applies to a pumping, because publishing a 6% number and invoicing 21% is exactly the practice named as a scam in `research/02` 4.5.

### 5. Reading the market

The field splits into two clean groups and the split is the whole story.

- **The firms that publish real, conditioned terms sit at 7 days to 4 months:** M&S 7 days on a standard unblocking, Pro-Per 30 days, Clean Débouchage 30 days, Riooldienst 2 months, De Rioolkrak 120 days.
- **The firms advertising 12 to 24 months publish no conditions whatsoever:** SaniChauffe 12 months, Mertens "up to 2 years", Monsieur le Déboucheur "up to 24 months". The word "jusqu'à" and the absence of any exclusion list is the tell. A 24 month guarantee on a drain that the customer keeps putting wet wipes into is not a promise anyone intends to honour.
- **The mechanism everyone who thinks about it uses is the same:** a plain unblocking gets a short guarantee, a full curage gets a long one, because after a curage the company has actually seen and cleaned the whole line. M&S sells it that way, Debouchage77 says it in words, De Rioolkrak's 10 year term applies only to works, never to an unblocking.
- **Two conditions recur and are worth copying:** the invoice must be paid, and the guarantee does not apply when the technician noted on the spot that the pipe itself is the problem (broken, collapsed, root-invaded, too flat).

**Market-standard duration, honestly read: 1 month on a straightforward unblocking, 3 to 12 months when a full curage was done.** Anything longer is only offered by firms that publish no conditions, and AGENTS.md rule 1 rules that out for us.

### 6. PROPOSED guarantee for Pro Débouchage

Short version for the page and the cards.

> **FR:** Garantie 1 mois sur le débouchage. Si la même canalisation se rebouche dans les 30 jours, nous revenons gratuitement.
>
> **NL:** Garantie 1 maand op de ontstopping. Raakt dezelfde leiding binnen 30 dagen opnieuw verstopt, dan komen wij gratis terug.

Full version for the conditions block, so the short line is never a half-truth.

> **FR:** Garantie 1 mois sur le débouchage, et 6 mois si nous avons fait un curage complet de la conduite. La garantie couvre la partie de la conduite que nous avons débouchée. Elle ne couvre pas les lingettes, les graisses, les racines, un affaissement ou une conduite cassée. Si le technicien voit que la conduite elle-même est en cause, il vous le dit et l'écrit sur le bon de travail avant de partir.
>
> **NL:** Garantie 1 maand op de ontstopping, en 6 maanden als wij de leiding volledig gereinigd hebben. De garantie geldt voor het stuk leiding dat wij ontstopt hebben. Ze geldt niet bij vochtige doekjes, vet, wortels, verzakking of een gebroken buis. Ziet de technieker dat de leiding zelf het probleem is, dan zegt hij het en schrijft hij het op de werkbon voor hij vertrekt.

Why these numbers. **1 month matches every competitor that publishes conditions** (30 days at Pro-Per and Clean Débouchage, 2 months at Riooldienst, 120 days at De Rioolkrak), so it is defensible and cheap to honour. **6 months after a curage** is the mechanism the serious firms use, sits between M&S's 3 months on a classic curage and its 1 year on a general one, and gives the 249 curage card a real reason to exist. The exclusion list is copied from the two firms that publish one, and writing it on the work order is De Rioolkrak's condition, which is also the thing that protects Afrem from a customer who flushes wipes.

One judgement call, flagged as Fady's own and not something the files decided: **Pro Débouchage should not write "jusqu'à 24 mois"** even though two competitors in the zone do. It reads bigger and it converts, but it is unbacked by any condition, it collides with AGENTS.md rule 1, and the whole positioning of this page is that we are the honest one on a street full of bait numbers.
