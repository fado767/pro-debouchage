# Brussels, the public facts (2026-09-27, Sonnet web agent, no Chrome)

Asked by Fady on 2026-09-27: how important would it be to include the 19 Brussels communes, would it bring more jobs, on data and not on assumptions. This file holds the PUBLIC facts only, each with its source. No opinion on the decision. Marks: CONFIRMED (official or primary page), SECONDARY (press, a company page, a search summary), NO SOURCE FOUND. Filed by the session from the agent's report, shortened, nothing added.

- Flemish Brabant: 1,204,541 (2025) or 1,196,773 (1 Jan 2024), two figures, both SECONDARY. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemish_Brabant
- Walloon Brabant: about 414,130 (1 Jan 2024), SECONDARY. https://www.citypopulation.de/en/belgium/brabantwallon/
- Belgium: 11,825,551 on 1 Jan 2025, CONFIRMED. https://statbel.fgov.be/en/news/01-january-2025-belgium-had-11825551-inhabitants
- Towns outside the two Brabants (all SECONDARY): Aalst 92,066, Dendermonde 47,532, Ninove 40,580 (1 Jan 2025); Mechelen 88,614 (2023); Willebroek 27,081 (2021); Boom 19,699 (2025); Kontich about 21,200 (2020 or 2021).

## 2. Housing stock
- Dwellings built before 1946: Brussels 65.79 percent, Wallonia 48.42, Flanders 26.76 (SECONDARY, L'Avenir on the Statbel census 2021, 19 Oct 2021). A second, loosely sourced figure says 60 percent for Brussels: the two disagree. https://www.lavenir.net/regions/2021/10/19/infographies-decouvrez-si-votre-maison-est-plus-vieille-que-la-moyenne-belge-et-regionale-DCG5VEBAHBB75FB5AO3Z5SHUKA/
- Single houses as a share of dwellings, census 2021: Flanders 71 percent, Wallonia 74, Brussels 15. So about 85 percent of Brussels dwellings sit in multi-unit buildings (CONFIRMED, Statbel). https://statbel.fgov.be/fr/themes/census/logement/type-de-logement
- Tenure, census 2021: Brussels 62 percent tenants, 38 percent owner-occupiers, the only region with more tenants than owners; Flanders about 70 percent owners, Wallonia about 64 (CONFIRMED, Statbel and IBSA). https://ibsa.brussels/actualites/62-pourcents-de-locataires-en-rbc
- Inside Brussels: tenants dominate in the centre (Saint-Gilles, Ixelles, Saint-Josse, City of Brussels, Etterbeek); owners are close to or above tenants in the north-west (Jette, Ganshoren, Berchem-Sainte-Agathe, Koekelberg) and the south (Uccle, Woluwe-Saint-Pierre, Auderghem, Watermael-Boitsfort) (CONFIRMED, same release).
- Co-ownership and syndics, share of dwellings: NO SOURCE FOUND.
- No source links the age of the housing to the number of drain problems. None is inferred here.

## 3. Parking for tradesmen, 2026
- "Carte de dérogation intervention": 75 EUR a year, valid in all 19 communes, shown with a parking disc and an "intervention in progress" notice. NOT valid in red and orange zones. Meant for trades called out for emergencies, plumbers named (CONFIRMED, parking.brussels, read Sept 2026). https://parking.brussels/en/professional-regional-parking-permit and https://parking.brussels/en/faq-carte-derogation-professionel
- The separate "carte professionnelle" needs a company mobility plan and is valid only in the company's own commune (CONFIRMED). https://www.parking.brussels/fr/carte-professionnelle
- How long the request takes: NO SOURCE FOUND.
- Without a card, City of Brussels red zone: 9.20 EUR for the 2-hour maximum (SECONDARY). Flat fine for not paying: 37 EUR in the City of Brussels, Saint-Josse and Uccle; Etterbeek "parking minute" zones 100 EUR per half day (SECONDARY, commune pages). It differs per commune.

## 4. Low emission zone (the whole Region)
- Since 1 Jan 2026: diesel Euro 5 and older and petrol Euro 2 and older are banned. A diesel van needs at least Euro 6, a petrol van at least Euro 3 (CONFIRMED, Bruxelles Environnement). https://environnement.brussels/citoyen/news/2026/lez-ce-qui-change-depuis-le-1er-janvier-2026
- The postponement to 2027 (ordinance of 21 March 2025) was suspended by the Constitutional Court on 11 Sept 2025 and annulled on 11 Dec 2025, so the 2026 date held (CONFIRMED by several dated press and legal sources). https://www.lalibre.be/regions/bruxelles/2025/12/11/la-cour-constitutionnelle-annule-le-report-de-la-lez-a-bruxelles-ce-que-cela-change-pour-les-automobilistes-BXRUEW7VLVGENAMWY6CARR77GQ/
- Enforcement 2026: a first offence gets a warning letter; a new offence from 3 months later can cost 350 EUR. Fines under this procedure are sent from 1 July 2026, for offences from 23 June 2026 (CONFIRMED, same official page).
- Day pass for a non-compliant car or van: 35 EUR, at most 24 days a year per plate (CONFIRMED). https://lez.brussels/mytax/fr/day-pass-info
- An annual pass of 350 EUR and an 80 EUR monthly option are named by one press source only: UNRESOLVED, not confirmed on an official page. https://www.rtbf.be/article/lez-a-bruxelles-les-amendes-entreront-en-vigueur-le-7-juin-un-pass-annuel-a-350-euros-adopte-11705117

## 5. Competition and the prices customers see
- One directory (BlueBook) lists 7 companies for "débouchage égouts Bruxelles", none with a fixed price. One listing, not a census (SECONDARY). https://www.bluebook.be/debouchage-egouts-bruxelles.htm
- One company advertises "à partir de 20 euros" on its own site (SECONDARY, self-published). https://www.debouchages.brussels/
- SPF Economie warning, relayed by L'Avenir on 16 Oct 2025: complaints about débouchage websites that trace back to three companies under many site names, advertising from 59 EUR, a price the SPF calls fictional, the real price much higher (CONFIRMED as an SPF communication relayed by press). https://www.lavenir.net/actu/belgique/2025/10/16/des-services-de-debouchage-et-de-plomberie-juges-suspects-par-le-spf-economie-les-entreprises-accusees-de-pratiques-commerciales-prejudiciables-WMF2AV7IVBEQROMAEGAJ547224/
- Same season: 17 emergency repairers on trial for fraud, and consumer pieces on dishonest plumbers in RTBF and Moustique (SECONDARY, Sept and Oct 2025).
