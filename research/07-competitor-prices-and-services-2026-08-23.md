## 2. The computed market picture (daytime, weekday)

Medians are computed on the published figures above. Where a source is HTVA it is converted at **6%** (the rate that applies to a home in use for more than 10 years) so everything below is comparable **TVAC**.

| Job | Low | Median | High | Read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WC bouché | 60 | **about 105** | 185 | Half the field lands between 75 and 120 |
| Évier, lavabo, douche | 60 | **about 95** | 155 | The cheapest job in every grid |
| Canalisation intérieure au furet | 69 | **about 115** | 190 | |
| Égout, riool, sterput, main line | 89 | **about 180** | 265 | The NL market standard "140 to 200 incl. VAT" sits right on this |
| Hydrocurage, haute pression | 106 | **about 155** | 265 | Heavy truck jobs run past 600 and nobody prices those online |
| Camera inspection, standalone | 95 | **about 145** | 350 | A written report is a paid extra almost everywhere: plus 50 to plus 100 |
| Camera added to an unblocking | 100 | **about 106** | 120 | Speedy plus 100, Ontstoppingsservice plus 100, RIOOL Team plus 100 |
| Fosse septique, septische put | 127 | **about 189** | 210 | Or about 120 per m3 (Turbo). L'Expert: 150 to 195 HTVA for a 5 m3 job |
| Pompage cave inondée | np | **np** | np | Confirmed again today: **nobody in the zone publishes a price for this** |

**Typical surcharge pattern.** Where the market publishes at all it is remarkably consistent: **evening from 18h or 19h and Saturday plus 50%, night from 22h and Sunday or holidays plus 100%.** Speedy, Ontstoppingsservice, SaniChauffe and Débouchage BW all use that shape. Debouchage77 expresses the same thing as fixed call-out steps (75 day, 90 evening, 130 night and weekend). L'Expert prices a night job at about 1.6 times its day job. Everyone else says "supplement by phone", which is exactly the ambiguity customers fear (`research/02` 4.4).

**Typical call-out.** Three patterns. (a) Included in the forfait: Mertens, DCM, Speedy, Ontstoppingsservice, and L'Expert within 20 km. (b) A small deductible fee: SaniChauffe 30, deducted if the job goes ahead. (c) A real standalone charge: Debouchage77 75 by day, Turbo about 60. **Almost nobody says what happens if nothing gets fixed.** The only honest published answer in the whole set is Speedy and Ontstoppingsservice: 95 by day, 185 at night or weekend, charged only once the technician has arrived. That silence is a gap we can take.

**VAT.** The field is split roughly half HTVA, half TVAC, and several sites state neither, which makes a "from 60" unreadable. In the zone only Brupro, SaniChauffe and Débouchage BW commit to TVAC.

## 3. Most demanded services, in order

Ranked by how often a service is pushed first across the sites in this file plus the sixteen in `research/02` section 3, cross-checked against the search terms in `research/02` 4.2.

1. **Urgent unblocking of an indoor drain: WC, évier, douche.** Pushed first by nearly every site, and it carries the highest-intent searches (WC bouché, verstopte wc, évier bouché, verstopte gootsteen). This is the money.
2. **Égout, riool, sterput and chambre de visite.** Second on almost every site, bigger ticket, and the term the dedicated Vilvoorde and Grimbergen landing pages are built on (ontstoppingsdienst Vilvoorde, débouchage égout).
3. **Hydrocurage and hogedrukreiniging.** The upsell attached to job 2, and the planned-work search (curage, onderhoud, preventief).
4. **Camera inspection.** Present in every service list, priced by only a third, and increasingly its own search (camera-inspectie riool, inspection caméra canalisation). Roro ranks it first commercially, the market ranks it fourth by push. Both are true: it is low volume and high trust.
5. **Fosse septique and septische put.** Steady, planned, priced by four sites.
6. **Pompage cave inondée.** Event-driven, unpriced by literally everyone, and therefore the least contested and the easiest to own with a real number.

## 4. PROPOSAL for Roro (not his price until he says yes)

**Everything in this section is Fady's proposal, computed from section 2. Roro confirms, changes or refuses every figure. Nothing goes on any page before he answers.**

Positioning chosen: **at or just above the market median, never at the bait end.** Roro said he does not want to rip people off but wants to charge reasonably. The bait end (52, 60, 69) is exactly where the complaints in `research/02` 4.4 and 4.5 come from, and matching it would force the same behaviour we are selling against. Sitting on the median with an all-in number that never moves is the honest version of the same promise, and it stays under the honest reference point quoted in the Moustique investigation (about 165 EUR per hour maximum for drain clearing).

### The three "this exact job costs X" cards

| Card | The job, as the customer says it | PROPOSED price | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **WC bouché / Verstopte wc** | **129 EUR TVAC** | Déplacement included, first hour included, furet and machine included. Day rate, Monday to Friday 7h to 18h. Median is about 105, so this sits above it, and what the extra buys is a number that never changes |
| 2 | **Évier, lavabo ou douche bouché / Verstopte gootsteen, lavabo of douche** | **119 EUR TVAC** | Same inclusions, same hours. Median about 95. Deliberately 10 EUR under card 1 because the job really is shorter |
| 3 | **Égout ou sterput bouché, haute pression / Verstopte riool of sterput, hogedruk** | **199 EUR TVAC** | Déplacement, the jetting machine and the first hour included, plus **a camera check of the cleared line included**. Median about 180, so this is a small premium, justified because competitors add about 100 for the camera |

Why these three: cards 1 and 2 are rank 1 of section 3, split in two because the customer searches for the fixture and not for "débouchage", and card 3 is rank 2, the bigger ticket and the term the local landing pages fight over. Camera-only, fosse septique and pompage stay off the cards and get a "sur devis, prix annoncé avant le départ du technicien" line, because their real cost varies too much to promise one number honestly.

### The PROPOSED surcharge line, published on the page

> Soir 18h à 22h et samedi: plus 50%. Nuit 22h à 7h, dimanche et jours fériés: plus 75%. Le supplément vous est dit au téléphone avant que le technicien parte.
> Avond 18u tot 22u en zaterdag: plus 50%. Nacht 22u tot 7u, zondag en feestdagen: plus 75%. Het supplement wordt aan de telefoon gezegd voor de technieker vertrekt.

Reasoning: plus 50% evening and Saturday is exactly the market norm, so it is uncontroversial. **The night and Sunday figure is deliberately plus 75% where the market is plus 100%**, which makes Pro Débouchage measurably cheaper at three in the morning than every competitor that publishes anything. Card 1 at night becomes 226 EUR TVAC against roughly 258 at a plus 100% competitor. Only two of sixteen competitors publish any surcharge at all, so publishing it is itself the differentiator (`research/02` 3.3, gap 2).

### The PROPOSED camera inspection price

- **Camera inspection alone, no blockage: 149 EUR TVAC**, including the written report with photos and video, and including the version an insurer accepts. Standalone median is about 145, so this is on the market. The report being included is the differentiator: Speedy charges plus 50 for it, Ontstoppingsservice plus 75, and `research/02` 4.1 found plus 75 to plus 100 as the norm.
- **Free when it comes with a paid unblocking job**, which is Roro's own USP (`business-brief.md` section 1). Worth stating as a euro figure on the page, "inspection caméra offerte, valeur 149 EUR", rather than the vague "gratuit".

### Two things Roro must settle before any of this is published

1. **The VAT line.** Every figure above is TVAC at **6%**, the rate for a home in use for more than 10 years, which is most of this zone. At 21% card 1 becomes 143 and card 3 becomes 221. Charging 21% where 6% applies is one of the named scam practices (`research/02` 4.5), so the page needs one plain line saying which rate applies to you and why. Roro confirms with his accountant that 6% is what he applies on these jobs.
2. **What happens if nothing gets fixed.** Nobody local answers this and it is the cheapest trust win available. Proposal: **if the technician arrives and cannot unblock it, the customer pays 60 EUR TVAC for the trip and nothing else, and this is said on the phone before he leaves.** Market comparison: 95 by day at Speedy and Ontstoppingsservice. Roro has to agree he can live with that before it is written anywhere.
