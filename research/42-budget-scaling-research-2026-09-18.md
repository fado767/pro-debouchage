# research/42, what a 20 to 30 EUR budget raise does, and the Google rep calls (2026-09-18, Sonnet research agent, WebSearch and WebFetch, no login)

*Raw agent report, condensed by the session. STRONG means Google's own documentation or a dated study, OPINION means practitioner writing. Question put to it: Roro wants 30 EUR a day and the Dutch back, what does the documentation say.*

## 1. Budget change and learning
- STRONG: Google's learning-period page names a new bid strategy, a bid-strategy setting change and a composition change (campaigns, ad groups, keywords added or removed) as triggers. It does NOT mention budget changes or any percentage. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13020501
- OPINION: the "15 to 20 percent at a time, every 3 to 5 days" rule is practitioner lore aimed at Target CPA and Target ROAS, not a Google figure (Hawksem, Groas). Maximize Clicks is not conversion-based, so a budget change is the least likely to destabilise it.
- STRONG: Google only says to minimise budget changes on a running campaign and warns of uneven spend while it re-paces. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10487143
- STRONG, overdelivery: a campaign "might spend up to twice your average daily budget" on a day, and in a month never more than 30.4 times the daily budget. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1704443 . At 30 EUR a day: a single day can reach 60, the monthly ceiling is 912 EUR. At 20 it is 608.

## 2. What budget buys when the loss is to rank
- STRONG: lost IS (budget) is the share of time ads were not shown for lack of budget; lost IS (rank) is the share lost to poor Ad Rank. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7103314
- With about 72 percent lost to rank and about 17 to budget, extra budget buys more clicks on auctions already being won and nothing on the auctions lost on rank. OPINION (Adalysis, RebootIQ): the levers for rank loss come first: bid cap, Quality Score parts (expected CTR, ad relevance, landing page experience), tighter ad-to-keyword relevance; budget last.

## 3. Two languages
- STRONG: language targeting is a campaign-level setting, ad groups cannot carry their own. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722078 . The agent also reports that the same page announces the removal of campaign-level language targeting in Search from September 2026, with ads matched to the query language automatically: NOT verified by the session, check it in the account before relying on it.
- OPINION (PPC Hero, the Ads community): one campaign per language with its own budget is the consensus, because budget, bid strategy and language cannot be split inside a shared campaign. The agent's own view: at 3 to 4 clicks a day two budgets of 10 to 15 EUR risk starving both; re-enabling NL inside the one campaign and reading it separately is the pragmatic path.

## 4. The "Google Ads specialist" calls
- OPINION (SterlingSky, Nowspeed): a mix of Google outreach staff and outsourced vendors calling on Google's behalf. There is no obligation to take the meeting.
- STRONG: Google's own page says to verify by a follow-up e-mail from an @google.com or @ads.google.com address and to check links independently. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2375460
- OPINION: no account setting opts out; the way out is to say on the call that you do not want to be contacted (BizIQ). What such calls push: broad match, Performance Max, auto-applied recommendations, budget raises; Optmyzr calls broad match without strong conversion signals "expensive guesswork" for small advertisers. No Belgian regulator source was found.

## 5. Low-volume call accounts
- OPINION (Marin Software): Maximize Clicks for the data-gathering phase, Smart Bidding needs a conversion volume this account does not have.
- STRONG: the call-length threshold is a setting (default 60 seconds) and Google says to set it from real call history; imported call conversions exist as a feature. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6100664
- OPINION (WhatConverts, Business Focus): a missed call that the owner phones back from his own mobile gets zero attribution; honest measurement is a manual log of every real job with its source and value, matched to click days.

## 6. Ad scheduling
- STRONG: schedule bid adjustments work under Maximize Clicks; Smart Bidding ignores them. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6372644 and https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2732132
- The agent's view: 24/7 business, 3 to 4 clicks a day, hour-of-day tweaks would fragment a thin dataset. Not yet.

## The agent's six-line summary
1. FACT: no documented learning reset from a budget change alone; Maximize Clicks is the least exposed.
2. FACT: at 30 EUR a day a single day can hit 60 and the monthly ceiling is 912 EUR. Tell the owner the ceiling.
3. FACT: with most loss to rank, the extra 10 EUR buys more of the auctions already won, not the lost ones.
4. OPINION: Quality Score, bid cap and ad or landing relevance first, budget last.
5. FACT: language targeting is campaign-level, so FR plus NL in one campaign is a structural constraint; splitting thins the volume at this scale.
6. OPINION: 30 EUR is reasonable and low-risk, and it is "more of the same", not a fix for rank.
