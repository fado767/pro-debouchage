# 26-belgian-invoice-legal-musts-2026-08-27.md

*Every legal must for PRO DEBOUCHAGE SRL invoices, researched 2026-08-27 (Opus agent, overnight) for the invoice-template build. Grounded in the SPF Finances / fisconetplus consolidated VAT royal decrees (updated to the AR of 14.02.2026). The exact legal wordings below are copied verbatim and must never be paraphrased on the invoice. Owner of decisions: the accountant for the UNCERTAIN rates, DECISIONS.md for ours.*

## 1. A B2C invoice is ALWAYS mandatory in this trade

AR no 1 art. 1 + art. 20 par. 2: every "travail immobilier" (art. 19 par. 2 CTVA: construction, transformation, reparation, entretien, nettoyage, demolition of an immovable) must be invoiced even to a private individual. **No threshold, no cash-job exemption.** A 150 euro night call-out gets an invoice. Deadline: at the latest the 15th of the month after the month of the work (or of cashing an advance). Fines for missing/late invoices: 50 euro first offence rising to 5,000 (AR no 44).

## 2. Mandatory mentions

**VAT (AR no 1, art. 5 par. 1):** invoice date; a unique SEQUENTIAL number (per-year series like 2026-0001 is fine, no gaps, credit notes may have their own series); supplier name, seat address and VAT number; customer name and address (plus the customer's VAT number in B2B, compulsory); the intervention date whenever it differs from the invoice date; a real description with quantities (never one line "travaux"; include the dwelling address, it justifies the rate); unit prices excl. VAT and the taxable base PER RATE; the rate(s) and total VAT; on later invoices a reference to any acompte invoice.

**Company law (CSA art. 2:20):** legal name exactly as in the statutes; the form (SRL on FR documents, BV on NL); the seat; the enterprise number; **"RPM" plus the court of the seat**; e-mail and website where they exist. For the Vilvoorde seat the court is the Nederlandstalige Ondernemingsrechtbank Brussel, so **"RPM Bruxelles" (FR) / "RPR Brussel" (NL)**. Confirm against the BCE extract.

**Formats:** enterprise number XXXX.XXX.XXX; VAT number BE + the same 10 digits (ours starts with 1: BE 1027.454.187). Bank account not legally required but expected: IBAN + BIC.

**Simplified invoices (under 100 euro): do not use in this business** (they cannot carry the 6 percent justification and are excluded for reverse-charge work).

## 3. THE 6 PERCENT SENTENCE (print verbatim, never abridge)

Since 01.01.2022 the client attestation is replaced by this declaration ON the invoice (AR no 20, tableau A, rubrique XXXI, par. 1, 5). The presumption only works with the full text; short forms circulating in invoicing tools do not count. Client contestation window: ONE MONTH from receipt, in writing; absent contestation (and absent collusion), the supplier's rate liability is discharged.

**FR (official):**
"Taux de T.V.A.: En l'absence de contestation par écrit, dans un délai d'un mois à compter de la réception de la facture, le client est présumé reconnaître que (1) les travaux sont effectués à un bâtiment d'habitation dont la première occupation a eu lieu au cours d'une année civile qui précède d'au moins dix ans la date de la première facture relative à ces travaux, (2) qu'après l'exécution de ces travaux, l'habitation est utilisée, soit exclusivement soit à titre principal comme logement privé et (3) que ces travaux sont fournis et facturés à un consommateur final. Si au moins une de ces conditions n'est pas remplie, le taux normal de T.V.A. de 21 p.c. sera applicable et le client endossera, par rapport à ces conditions, la responsabilité quant au paiement de la taxe, des intérêts et des amendes dus."

**NL (official):**
"Btw-tarief: Bij gebrek aan schriftelijke betwisting binnen een termijn van één maand vanaf de ontvangst van de factuur, wordt de klant geacht te erkennen dat (1) de werken worden verricht aan een woning waarvan de eerste ingebruikneming heeft plaatsgevonden in een kalenderjaar dat ten minste tien jaar voorafgaat aan de datum van de eerste factuur met betrekking tot die werken, (2) de woning, na uitvoering van die werken, uitsluitend of hoofdzakelijk als privéwoning wordt gebruikt en (3) de werken worden verstrekt en gefactureerd aan een eindverbruiker. Wanneer minstens één van die voorwaarden niet is voldaan, zal het normale btw-tarief van 21% van toepassing zijn en is de afnemer ten aanzien van die voorwaarden aansprakelijk voor de betaling van de verschuldigde belasting, interesten en geldboeten."

No official EN version exists; an EN invoice still carries the FR or NL text (translation alongside is allowed). The 6 percent rate itself is unchanged in the 14.02.2026 consolidation. Conditions: dwelling 10+ years (measured against the FIRST invoice for the works, acompte included), used mainly as private home, invoiced to the final consumer, and the works are transformation, renovation, reparation or entretien "à l'exclusion du nettoyage".

## 4. B2B reverse charge (autoliquidation)

For a Belgian VAT-registered business customer filing periodic returns, no rate and no VAT amount appear; instead this exact sentence (AR no 1 art. 20 par. 3, wording since 01.01.2023):

"Autoliquidation : En l'absence de contestation par écrit, dans un délai d'un mois à compter de la réception de la facture, le client est présumé reconnaître qu'il est un assujetti tenu au dépôt de déclarations périodiques. Si cette condition n'est pas remplie, le client endossera, par rapport à cette condition, la responsabilité quant au paiement de la taxe, des intérêts et des amendes dus."

Syndic nuance: an association des copropriétaires (ACP/VME) is normally NOT a VAT taxable person, so no reverse charge: charge VAT to the ACP. Whether an ACP counts as "consommateur final" for 6 percent on common parts is widely accepted practice but was not confirmed by an official Belgian text in this search: verify with the accountant.

## 5. Which rate per service (SPF table + doctrine)

The official SPF table (fin.belgium.be, "Taux de TVA applicable pour certains types de travaux") names at 6 percent, verbatim: "ramonage des cheminées, débouchage des égouts et des canalisations". At 21 percent: household cleaning of the dwelling.

| Service | Rate | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Débouchage (10+ year private home, final consumer) | 6 | SOLID, named in the SPF table |
| Repair of pipes/sanitary | 6 | SOLID |
| Curage / hydrocurage | 6, wording caveat | MEDIUM: the statute excludes "nettoyage"; bill it as "curage/débouchage de canalisation", NEVER as "nettoyage" |
| Vidange fosse septique alone | commonly 21 | UNCERTAIN, sources disagree, no official text; accountant in writing before the first septic invoice |
| Camera alone as diagnosis | probably 21 | UNCERTAIN (the "travaux intellectuels" 21 doctrine was written for architects/engineers) |
| Camera as part of a 6 percent job, same invoice | 6 | MEDIUM-HIGH, accessory to the principal operation; keep the camera line subordinate |
| Cellar pumping alone | likely 21 | UNCERTAIN; arguably 6 as preparation for a 6 percent repair on the same invoice |
| Under-10-years, non-residential or not final consumer | 21 | SOLID |

Mixed invoices: taxable base, rate and VAT shown PER RATE, two lines, never averaged.

## 6. Late payment, B2C (Livre XIX CDE, since 01.09.2023)

First reminder FREE, then at least 14 calendar days before anything accrues (count from the third working day after postal sending, or the day after electronic). The reminder must state: balance, the indemnity that will apply, creditor name and enterprise number, what the debt is for and when it fell due, and the 14-day line. Caps, only if stipulated in the conditions BEFOREHAND: interest at the reference rate + 8 points; flat indemnity max 20 euro (balance up to 150), 30 euro + 10 percent of the excess (150.01 to 500), 65 euro + 5 percent of the excess above 500, absolute cap 2,000. Nothing on top; excess clauses are void. Our CGV already carry the compliant clause with the mirror in the consumer's favour (research/22).

## 7. Data and retention

On the invoice: name, address, B2B VAT number, intervention address (justifies the rate). NEVER: national register number, birth date, ID number, card details, photos, free-text notes about the customer. **Retention: 10 years** from 1 January after the invoice date (art. 60 CTVA as amended 2022, and art. 315 CIR92; the accounting law's 7 years is the shorter clock and does not govern). An erasure request moves invoice data to restricted archive until the 10 years pass.

## 8. The bon pour accord belongs on the WORK ORDER, never the invoice

A waiver signed after the work is worthless. The work order (signed before or at the start) carries: the parties, the description of the urgent intervention requested, the price or pricing basis, the customer's EXPRESS request for immediate execution, his express acknowledgement that full performance ends the withdrawal right (art. VI.53 8 and its VI.73 mirror; both elements cumulative), CGV acceptance, and "Bon pour accord" + date + signature. The exact FR and NL waiver wordings are in research/22 section 2. Extras beyond the urgent repair revive the withdrawal right for those extras. The "mention manuscrite" rules circulating online are French law, not Belgian: the signature is what counts.

## 9. Peppol status (August 2026)

Since 01.01.2026 structured e-invoicing (Peppol BIS UBL over the Peppol network) is MANDATORY for domestic B2B between Belgian VAT-registered businesses (AR no 1 art. 13ter/13quater). A PDF e-mailed to a business customer no longer satisfies the duty. **B2C is NOT affected: paper or PDF stays legal for homeowners**, which is the bulk of this business. Receiving structured invoices must be technically possible regardless (fines 1 to 5,000 euro); the company should be findable on Peppol under its enterprise number. The wife already sends B2B via Peppol, so that side is compliant; ACPs that are not VAT taxable stay outside the mandate.

## 10. Layout conventions and special documents

Header: logo + the full CSA 2:20 identity block + FACTURE title + number, date, due date. Customer block with "Lieu d'intervention" on its own line when it differs. One line per operation with quantity and unit price excl. VAT. Bottom right the three-column recap (Base imposable | Taux | Montant TVA) then Total HTVA, Total TVA, TOTAL TVAC (NL: Maatstaf van heffing | Tarief | Btw-bedrag; Totaal excl. btw, Totaal btw, TE BETALEN incl. btw). Footer: the 6 percent declaration in full (small type is fine, complete and legible), payment details with structured communication, the conditions reference.

Credit notes: separate document, own series (NC-2026-001), references the original invoice, and when the original bore VAT carries verbatim "TVA à reverser à l'État dans la mesure où elle a été initialement déduite" (NL: "Btw terug te storten aan de Staat in de mate waarin ze oorspronkelijk in aftrek werd gebracht"). Never delete or overwrite an issued invoice.

Acomptes: same mentions incl. the 6 percent declaration; VAT due on CASHING; final invoice references and deducts them; the 10-year age test runs against the FIRST invoice, acompte included.

Cash: 3,000 euro ceiling per operation or linked operations (loi 18.09.2017 art. 67; fines up to 10 percent of the cash amount). Recommended footer line: "Paiement en espèces limité à 3.000 EUR (art. 67, loi du 18.09.2017)." Cash jobs still get invoiced, always (section 1).
