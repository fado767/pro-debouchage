# Tri-language conversion copy audit (2026-08-24, Opus agent, read-only)

*Raw agent report, filed by the 2026-08-24 session. The MECHANICAL entries were applied the same day by an editor agent; the JUDGEMENT entries wait on Fady. Sources read: AGENTS.md section 6, research/11, page-template.js (FR, NL), copy-en.js, meta-en.js, build-site.js META, business-brief.md.*

## A. Verdict per language

**French (fr-BE). Strong, close to shipping.** Register right for Belgium (sterput, chambre de visite, TVA comprise, narrow no-break spaces, Vilvorde). Systematic weakness: a voice drift between "on" (hero, steps, services; spoken, warm) and "nous" (prices, proof, zone, FAQ; formal), with the same sentence appearing in both voices. Second weakness: four near-miss idioms blurring the core promise ("Prix à l'avance", "avant que nous partions", "à la porte", "le prix honnête").

**Dutch (nl-BE). Genuinely Flemish, but visibly built on the French sentence.** All Belgian markers correct and deliberate (verplaatsingskosten, sterfput, lavabo, technieker, nazicht, verwittigen, proper, "Bel even"), "u" never drifts. Systematic weakness: French-shaped word choice, "interventie" nine times where a Fleming says beurt, klus, werk or nothing; two hard calques ("een uur van aankomst", "zijn btw inbegrepen"). One self-contradiction: finalL promised "geen toeslag" while notes[0] charges a toeslag (fixed same day).

**English (en-BE). Clear and accurate, but reads translated, and omits its own reason to exist.** Zero contractions, French declarative questions ("Your case is not in the list?"), and "before we leave" meaning the opposite of the promise. Strategic miss: the page exists for expats and EU staff and never says the call can be handled in English.
