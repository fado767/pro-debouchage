# research/44, how to win back the impressions lost to Ad Rank without paying more per click (2026-09-18, Sonnet research agent, web plus project files, read-only)

*Raw agent memo, condensed by the session. STRONG means Google's own documentation, OPINION means named practitioners. The problem put to it: about 72 percent of possible impressions lost to rank, Quality Scores 1 to 3, bid capped at 6 EUR where top positions cost 11 to 15.*

## 1. Ad Rank (STRONG, https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722122 and /answer/7634668)
- Ad Rank comes from the bid, the ad quality (expected CTR, ad relevance, landing page experience), the Ad Rank thresholds, the competitiveness of the auction, the context of the search, and the expected impact of ASSETS. Thresholds are higher for higher positions.
- With the bid capped, the only way up is the quality and assets side of the formula.

## 2. Quality Score (STRONG, /answer/6167118)
- Three components, each rated against other advertisers on the exact same search over the last 90 DAYS. Google calls it a diagnostic, not a KPI. OPINION (Optmyzr, Vallaeys): it is a lagging estimate built mostly on past exact-match history, so a three-week-old account reads low for thin history as much as for real faults. No named source says new domains are marked down as such.

## 3. Landing page experience (STRONG, /answer/2404197 and /answer/7543502)
- Google's factors: relevant original content, transparency (deliver what the ad promises, clear pricing), easy navigation; mobile speed is named as a lever. OPINION (Search Engine Land): a February 2025 update added navigational clarity and "unexpected destination" checks.
- What moves it: the H1 matching the keyword, the promised price and a tappable call action above the fold. SESSION NOTE: the consent card that covered the call button (research/35) was already fixed and deployed 2026-09-16/17; the ARRIVAL-TIME promise above the fold is still missing and waits on the window Roro can honestly keep (competitors sell 30 to 45 minutes).

## 4. Assets, the cheapest rank lever
- For a small local emergency trade: call asset, 6 or more sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, price assets, business name and logo, location asset; lead form low priority for a calls-only business.
- Business name and logo need completed advertiser verification (STRONG, /answer/12497613): this account has it, so both are available NOW. The location asset needs a verified Business Profile (STRONG, /answer/2404182).
- The account HAS: call asset, 4 sitelinks, 4 callouts and one structured snippet per group. MISSING: business name, business logo, price assets, promotion and message assets, the location asset (gated on the profile). Google's own recommendation cards flag the sitelinks and the logo (research/34 section 13). SESSION NOTE: price numbers in ads stay gated while VIES is INVALID (NOW.md week-4 agenda).

## 5. Responsive search ads (STRONG, /answer/6167122; OPINION, Adalysis)
- Google: a top keyword in at least two headlines but not all, 8 to 15 headlines, 3 to 4 descriptions, pin sparingly. Adalysis: Ad strength does not correlate with CTR; ads with 13 to 15 headlines get about three times the impressions of ads with 3 to 5.

## 6. Call-only ads (STRONG, /answer/16598240)
- Retired: no new call ads since February 2026, existing ones stop serving February 2027. RSA plus call asset, what the account runs, is the only path.

## 7. Local Services Ads (STRONG, support.google.com/localservices/answer/12174778 and /answer/6224841, Belgium)
- Available in Belgium, plumbers listed. Needs a verified public Business Profile, public liability insurance, a business check, a licence where the law asks one. LSA is migrating into Performance Max pay-per-lead, non-US markets in 2027 (/answer/17213585). Matches ads-program.md.

## 8. The 6 EUR cap (STRONG mechanism, /answer/6268626; the tactic is OPINION)
- Google: a cap controls cost, and a tight cap can force the campaign out of auctions a slightly higher bid would have won. Practitioner consensus: keep tight control early, work quality and assets first because they cost nothing, loosen the cap only on real conversion evidence, never pay more to cover a quality problem.
