# 19. The conversion playbook (landing-page masters)

*Date 2026-08-26, overnight v3 session. Agent: Claude, model Opus 5, web research. Condensed; the full
sourced version is in the session transcript. Applied to design/canvas-v3/.*

## Benchmarks

Plumbing Google Ads: ~10.49 USD CPC, 7.63% conversion, ~129 USD/lead (WordStream 2025). Dedicated
contractor landing pages 5-15%, homepages 1-3%; top decile >12% (Pipeline On). Target: 12%+ call rate;
under 6% means the page is broken, not the ads.

## The laws used (master behind each)

1. Rule of One (Joanna Wiebe): one reader, one promise, one action.
2. Attention ratio 1:1 (Oli Gardner): no nav, no outbound links, one goal.
3. Top 10% of the page confirms the search; the rest moves forward (Wiebe).
4. Message match is the highest-leverage change (lifts 66-212% documented). Six to ten hand-written
   per-ad-group H1 variants beat DKI, which breaks FR/NL grammar. (For the variants generator later.)
5. Write to Problem-Aware/Solution-Aware (Eugene Schwartz): no education, straight to "this one, now".
6. Clarity beats persuasion (Peep Laja).
7. Grunt test in 3 seconds (Donald Miller); customer is the hero, Afrem the guide; give the plan in
   3-4 steps.
8. Specifics beat generalities (Hopkins/Ogilvy).
9. Harry Dry's three tests: can they visualize it, can it be falsified, could a competitor paste it.
   Plus: periods over commas, no landing-page words, write how you talk.
10. Reading level ~grade 8; stress narrows processing.
11. Calm authority, not urgency stacking: state availability as fact ("we answer 24/7"), never pressure
    ("act now", countdowns). Strong prior, not a proven law; a legitimate later A/B.
12. The real objection is being ripped off; answer it above the fold (written price, registration,
    insurance).
13. Price transparency is a conversion feature in this category.
14. Click-to-call IS the conversion (calls convert ~25-40% vs ~2% forms). Sticky bar + 3-4 in-page CTAs;
    never two asks in one viewport.
15. Above the fold gets 57% of viewing time (NN/g); H1 + promise + CTA within the first screen.
16. Speed is copy: ~7%/second; LCP <2.5s, aim <1.8s.
17. Print only the response promise Roro can keep.
18. No reviews yet → substitute harder proof (real photos, enterprise number, insurance, price promise,
    named human); an empty review widget is worse than none.
19. WhatsApp = transitional CTA, job = photos, prefilled message; never equal weight with call.

## Hero blueprint chosen for v3

Problem in the customer's words stacked huge + answer on a marker stroke, sub carrying the price
promise, giant call button, micro-line "on décroche 24h/24", trust facts and real-photo strip below.
The text-only-above-the-fold variant (blueprint 5) remains the natural A/B against it.

## Section order rationale (as shipped)

Hero → trust facts → "your problem is on this list" (services) → the plan (4 steps) → price (in context,
surcharges published) → before/after proof → scam checklist (the market's #1 objection, answered point
by point) → who comes (identity after price: a tradesman prices first) → equipment → proof/photos +
honest reviews stance → situations (tenant/syndic/horeca) → zone → FAQ → final CTA → legal footer.

## Anti-slop copy tells to keep enforcing

No "solutions sur mesure / expertise reconnue / op maat / uw partner in"; no one-word triads
("Rapide. Fiable. Professionnel."); no adjective stacks nothing can falsify; no em dashes; every claim
answerable by Roro on the phone instantly.
