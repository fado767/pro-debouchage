# research/36, benchmarks for judging the campaign at day 19 (2026-09-16, Sonnet research agent, WebSearch and WebFetch, no login)

*Raw agent report, lightly cleaned. Question put to it: is it too early to judge, and what do successful emergency-service advertisers do differently. Every figure carries its source; US data unless stated. No Belgian, French or Dutch benchmark exists in public.*

## 1. Industry benchmarks (US-heavy)
- Plumbing search ads: CTR 4.97 percent, CPC 10.49 USD, conversion rate 7.63 percent, cost per lead 129.02 USD (LocaliQ 2025 home-services study; blends Google and Microsoft Ads, directional). LocaliQ flags plumbing as one of the LOWEST CTRs among home-services trades.
- Home services overall: CPC 7.85 USD, conversion rate 7.52 percent, down 14.97 percent year over year, CPC rising for 75 percent of advertisers (WordStream 2025).
- 2026 aggregator posts (Web Tonic, PPC Chief, TheEDigital): plumbing CPC 8.33 to 8.45 USD, conversion 6.1 to 11.3 percent (secondary compilations, weak).
- STRONG: mid-single-digit CTR (5 to 7 percent) and 7 to 11 percent conversion are the trade norm. Our 7 percent CTR and 8.45 percent tap rate are in line. Nothing here explains zero forwarding-number calls.

## 2. Google's own documentation
- Learning period (support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13020501): driven by conversion volume, cycle length and strategy; Smart Bidding can take up to about 50 conversion events or 3 conversion cycles to calibrate; no fixed day count; learning continues after the label clears.
- The "15 to 30 conversions a month" figure is practitioner consensus (Hawksem, KeywordMe), not Google's wording: Maximize Conversions "functions down to about 15 to 20 a month, performs best at 30 to 50".
- Phone call conversions (answer/6100664): calls from ads need CALL REPORTING enabled (it assigns a Google forwarding number); only calls above the merchant-set minimum duration count.
- Google forwarding numbers are available in Belgium (answer/2382961), toll-free and local, listed by country not by language. With 6 tap conversions and zero forwarding-number calls, the likely explanation is call reporting not on, not Belgium unsupported.

## 3. Statistics on small samples
- Wilson 95 percent interval for 6 taps in 60 clicks: about 4.7 to 20 percent (the agent's own calculation, standard method, no source). The true tap rate could be half or double the observed 10 percent.
- Adalysis: hold off reading ad-level CTR before roughly 1,000 impressions per ad; conversion-level test results need far more than three weeks of a new campaign. Practitioners: 50 to 100 clicks a week for three to four weeks before keyword-level conclusions (opinion).
- Bottom line: at 60 to 70 clicks and 19 days the account is statistically too young to declare the page, the keywords or the account a failure.

## 4. Local pack versus ads
- STRONG, dated: WebFX May 2026 (500 searches, 100 keywords across HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, garage doors): the Local Pack appeared in 94 percent of plumbing searches, Local Services Ads in 32 percent, classic Search ads in only 7 percent. Most demand in this vertical is captured by Maps visibility, which needs a verified Business Profile.
- Single source: Birdeye "State of Google Business Profile 2026": verified profiles drove about 12 percent more calls and 10 percent more direction requests than unverified ones.

## 5. Missed calls and call-back
- Inconsistent: CallRail (January 2025) 14 percent missed-call rate for home services; ServiceTitan (2024, 50,000 contractor lines) 62 percent; both cited second-hand (contractorincharge.com, getaira.io).
- Repeated across vendor-sponsored sources: up to 85 percent of unanswered callers do not call back; about 82 percent who reach voicemail call a competitor instead. Moderate confidence.
- NOT FOUND: any study of Belgian bilingual trades handling a Dutch caller on a French-speaking line; only vendor content on multilingual routing (Telsmart, Blarlo).

## 6. Market examples
- Belgian competitors sell arrival time and price transparency: GAM Débouchage Belgium (under 45 minutes in major cities), Déboucheur Express (30-minute arrival, price confirmed before work, travel included), Débouchage 77 (under 1 hour, a 4.9 out of 5 Google rating shown).
- US agency guidance (opinion): call-only or call-asset ads for emergency intent, structured snippets naming services, the number visible in the ad, review counts and stars on the landing page; one vendor claims 15 to 25 percent conversion for paired call-only ads plus landing pages (unverified marketing copy).
