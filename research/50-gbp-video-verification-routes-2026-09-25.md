# GBP video verification, routes after two failures

Read 2026-09-25. Web search and web fetch only, no Chrome. All claims carry a URL and the date read. Where nothing was found, it says "not found".

## 1. Google's official requirements

Source: [Verify your business with a video recording](https://support.google.com/business/answer/14271705?hl=en), read 2026-09-25.

- Minimum length: "at least 30 seconds long."
- Must be "an unedited, unique, and complete recording with no breaks," filmed live and uploaded directly on a mobile device inside the Business Profile flow. A video filmed elsewhere and uploaded later does not count (this last point per [Local Falcon guide](https://www.localfalcon.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-google-business-profile-video-verification), read 2026-09-25).
- Service-area businesses without a public address: "if you run a service-area business from your home, you can record landmarks and signs in your immediate neighborhood to verify the address." Required content: street identifiers and nearby landmarks, professional tools/equipment/branded items, and a business document (permit, invoice, utility bill) matching the profile name.
- Listed rejection examples on the page: business name not visible, no nearby area shown, missing proof of authorization to operate.
- No faces, no voices/background conversation of other people, no sensitive data (bank numbers, tax IDs, ID numbers) on camera (per [wiremo.co](https://wiremo.co/blog/google-business-profile-video-verification/), read 2026-09-25).
- Typical review time: about 5 business days (wiremo.co, same date).
- Not found: an exact second by second breakdown, or an official minimum outdoor duration.

## 2. What makes a "No view of surrounding area" retry pass

Source: [SterlingSky, Google Business Profile video verification](https://www.sterlingsky.ca/google-business-profile-video-verification/), read 2026-09-25.

- Fix stated directly: "start by showing the area around your business... show street signs, intersections, nearby businesses, or building numbers." For a storefront, show signage matching the profile name first.
- Recommended shot order: area around the business, then permanent signage/location proof, then workspace/operations, then tools/equipment/inventory, then branded assets, then vehicle/equipment access, then proof of access to the location, then business documents, then proof of system access.
- For a vehicle-based business: "open your work vehicle and show what is inside, such as tools or supplies", called "one of the strongest ways" to prove the business is real.
- Overall video length recommended "a few minutes," not the 30-second minimum. Not found: an exact number of outdoor seconds, or an explicit line that the van must be parked at the depot door (inferred from the location plus vehicle combination the guide recommends, not a quoted Google rule).
- Not found: whether Google requires the address spoken aloud, or a stated daylight rule (guides imply it for legibility, none quote Google on it).
- Nothing more specific for a depot/garage setup; the "home base" wording in source 1 is the only Google-side language for a hidden-address SAB.

## 3. Repeated failures: limits, lock, and the live video call option

- Not found: any official cap on the number of attempts, or a "profile locked" status name in an official Google source. Community threads describe only "failed multiple times" with no stated ceiling ([Google Business Profile Community thread](https://support.google.com/business/thread/367659927/video-verification-failed-multiple-times?hl=en), read 2026-09-25).
- Practitioner advice, not Google's own words: retry every 2 to 3 business days, on a different device and network if possible, up to about three tries, before escalating (source: search-engine-summarized guidance citing GBP community threads, read 2026-09-25).
- Live video call: exists as a Google Meet style call with an agent who reviews the setup in real time ([Brian Lawrence, GBP Live Video Call Verification](https://www.brianlawrence.com/live-video-call-verification/); [Intellar](https://intellar.agency/blog/google-business-profile-live-video-call-verification/), both read 2026-09-25). It is reported as not user-selectable: it does not normally appear as a menu option, and is switched on by Google, usually after repeated video failures and a support ticket, not requested directly.
- Belgium coverage: not found in any source read. No source names which countries carry the live call option; several explicitly say eligibility depends on "industry, location and account history" without listing countries.

## 4. The support route

- Path: support.google.com/business (or business.google.com/support) then "Contact us" (as opposed to "Post to the help community"). One summarized report describes typing "verification video upload fails" and choosing "Can't make or upload verification video" in the contact flow; this is a secondary paraphrase, not a quote from Google, so treat the exact wording as unconfirmed.
- Reported outcome: a ticket is opened, evidence can be uploaded and the problem described, and an agent reviews manually; some reports say Google then either activates the live video call option or calls the business directly.
- Documents: utility bills were removed as an accepted address verification document by Google in June 2025 ([Local Biz Guys](https://localbizguys.com/google-business-profiles-no-longer-accept-utility-bills-for-appeals/); [RevolutexDigital](https://revolutexdigital.com/google-business-verification-utility-bill-update/), both read 2026-09-25, describing a 2025 change, not reconfirmed for 2026). Reported as accepted instead: lease agreement, certificate of incorporation, business license/registration extract, tax registration documents, fire inspection report. A vehicle registration or insurance document was not found as an explicitly listed accepted document.
- Typical ticket turnaround: not found as a specific number of days; only the 5 business day figure for ordinary video review was found (see section 1).
- No source gave a specific "wording that works" for the ticket beyond the paraphrase above.

## 6. Other verification methods for a Belgian SAB in 2026

Source: [support.google.com/business/answer/7107242](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7107242?hl=en) and a 2026 methods summary, both read 2026-09-25.

- Methods listed for 2026: video, postcard/mail, phone or text code, email (to a business domain address), instant (via existing Search Console ownership of the domain), bulk (10+ locations, not applicable here).
- Postcard and phone/SMS are reported as not formally retired, but Google assigns the method, the business cannot pick one, and for hidden address SABs with a new profile video is reported as the default with little else offered.
- Email and instant verification do not apply here (no domain ownership route stated in the files).
- None of the alternative methods were reported by any source as currently offered to this profile; this is what is generally available in the product, not a confirmation of what Google will offer Pro Débouchage next.
