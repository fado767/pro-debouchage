## The Professional regional card, as the page words it
- For whom: "Toute entreprise (personne morale ou personne physique, association, société ou indépendant) active qui justifie sa nécessité de stationner régulièrement dans plusieurs commune de la Région de Bruxelles-Capitale dans le cadre de son métier."
- Where: "valable dans toute la Région de Bruxelles Capitale, en zone bleue, verte, grise et « événement ». Attention : la carte n'est pas valable dans les zones rouge, orange et « stationnement réservé »."
- To show: "Placez la carte de stationnement de manière visible et lisible derrière le pare-brise de votre véhicule."
- To pay: by transfer to IBAN BE53 0910 2169 1653, BIC GKCCBEBB, with the number plate as the message, or by bank card when the card is collected at an office (central office, Anderlecht, Ixelles, Jette, Molenbeek, Schaerbeek).
- The request: an online form, parking.brussels/fr/stationner-sa-voiture/carte-regionale-de-stationnement/demande-de-carte-regionale-de-stationnement-professionnel. Its first screen asks whether the company has named a person in charge of its parking cards and whether this is the first request. A first request needs "un plan de déplacement d'entreprise ou d'un équivalent".
- Renewal: the holder renews on his own initiative before the card ends ("Renouveler une carte" in the e-guichet). A change of plate or vehicle: parking.brussels/fr/demande-de-modification-de-carte-de-stationnement.

## Other products
- A communal "Professionnel" card, valid in ONE commune, price by commune. Example Anderlecht: 264 EUR a year, 74 a quarter, 26 a month for the first five cards.
- Without a card: street parking is paid by app, by SMS or at the meter; the rate depends on the zone and the commune; a free quarter of an hour exists with a 15-minute ticket.

## What it means for Roro (the session's arithmetic, not advice)
The card costs 90 EUR a month, jobs or no jobs. It does not cover red and orange zones, where he pays the meter anyway. No break-even can be computed from the files: the one street rate on file (research/52, 9.20 EUR for 2 hours in the City of Brussels) is a RED zone rate from a secondary source, and the zones the card covers cost less. The honest route: he starts by paying per job, counts his Brussels jobs and his parking cost for a month, then decides. His decision.
