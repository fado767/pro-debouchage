# The Brussels parking card, re-checked 2026-09-28: the 75 EUR figure is IN DOUBT

One Sonnet agent, web only, read-only. It could read parts of parking.brussels only: several pages are built by JavaScript and came back partial or as 404 to the fetch tool. So this file settles nothing yet. It records a contradiction with research/52 that must be settled on the official pages in a real browser, or by phone, BEFORE any guide goes to Roro and before anyone tells him a price.

## What research/52 said (27 Sep)
A yearly "intervention" card for emergency trades, 75 EUR a year, valid in all 19 communes, not in red and orange zones.

## What the re-check found
| Point | Found | Source, read 2026-09-28 |
|---|---|---|
| The card that fits a company working in several communes | "Carte régionale de stationnement Professionnel" (Professional regional parking permit), issued by parking.brussels | parking.brussels/fr/carte-regionale-de-stationnement-professionnel, parking.brussels/en/professional-regional-parking-permit |
| Its price | 1,080 EUR a year, or by the month from 90 EUR | the same page |
| What it allows | blue, green, grey and "event" zones in the whole region; NOT red, orange or reserved places; 2 hours at most per intervention, shown with a parking disc and a sign "en cours d'intervention" | the same page |
| Who may get it | any active business that shows a need to park in several Brussels communes for its work; no trade is named | the same pages |
| What the form asks | a named person in charge of the cards (a signed "procuration" if it is not the manager) and, for a first request, a company travel plan ("Plan de déplacements d'entreprise") or an accepted equivalent | parking.brussels/en/form/regional-pro |
| A 75 EUR a year card | the agent found that price only for the card of providers of non-urgent medical care at home, with the same zone rule and the same sign | search summaries quoting the parking.brussels FAQ, NOT read on the official page itself |
| A second, cheaper product | a "Carte professionnelle" valid in ONE commune; its price is not published on the page | parking.brussels/fr/carte-professionnelle |
| Contact | parking.brussels, Rue de l'Hôpital 31, 1000 Brussels, 02 563 39 00, Monday to Friday 8:30 to 12:30 | the contact page of parking.brussels |
| Low emission zone | a Euro 6 diesel van may drive in Brussels today; since 1 Jan 2026 Euro 5 diesel and older are banned | environnement.brussels (news of 2026 on the LEZ), lez.brussels |

## Not found or not confirmed
Whether an "intervention" card for emergency trades exists at all and at what price; whether a company seated outside Brussels (Vilvoorde) may apply; whether drain unblocking is covered; the sign-in method and the full list of documents; the time it takes; what happens when the van changes; the next date that changes anything for a Euro 6 diesel (2028 and 2030 appear on non-official sites only).
