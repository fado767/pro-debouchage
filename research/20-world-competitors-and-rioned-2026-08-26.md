## Belgium (our market): the corrections

**Publishing prices is NOT rare** (9 of 12 sites show figures) and **enterprise numbers are NOT absent**
(6 of 12 show one). Both are table stakes now. What NOBODY does: the price you actually PAY (all-in,
VAT stated, surcharge grid visible) plus a complete verifiable identity block plus a named human plus a
defined guarantee plus native bilingual copy. That combination is the leadership position: sell
CERTAINTY where everyone sells speed and is no longer believed.

Key sites: debouchage-rapide.be = strongest competitor (4.9/5, ~373-399 reviews, named B2B references:
McDonald's, Kiabi, an embassy; but no prices, no enterprise number, no written guarantee).
cleandebouchage.be = best written guarantee in Belgium (30 days, 180 with curage) + suspicious "temps
moyen 28 minutes". sos-debouchage.be = 3-month guarantee gutted by exclusions (wipes, grease, limescale,
foreign objects, curage, commercial, weekends: a warning template). debouchage-bxl.be = only FR site
publishing a real surcharge structure (+50% soir/sam, +100% dim/nuit). deboucheur-debouchage.be = most
granular grid TTC. debouchages.brussels = "àpde 20€" teaser (the scam-pattern price). VDD Vilvoorde
displays REMS/VIRAX/RIDGID logos; hydro-therm names a REMS 1200 Hydro: equipment branding exists
locally but only hand-tool brands, a van-mounted Rioned is a category above.

**Bilingual reality**: two Vilvoorde competitors have FR/NL toggles and both fail visibly (deweverguido:
machine translation, "A drain bouché", untranslated Dutch in the FR form; hydro-therm: "Vilvorde"
misspelled in its own URL, "sterput" left in FR copy, prices only on the NL side). The claim to own is
not "bilingual" but "each language reads as if it were the only one" (= our rule 6). Vilvoorde is
officially Dutch-speaking with ~33.7% French speakers (Koningslo, Beauval).

**Review depth is shallow in Brussels**: 20-30 genuine reviews would put us top-3. Review collection is
the highest-leverage post-launch task. Metro Rod's tree-per-review (ReviewForest) is a legitimate
incentive pattern.

**Distrust patterns to never repeat** (11): teaser prices, "à partir de" with nothing above, HTVA to
consumers, VAT omitted, contradictory numbers on one site, impossible counters, unprovable superlatives,
guarantees erased by exclusions, stock photos, mass city pages with emoji, claiming transparency while
hiding the number.

Google Local Services Ads with the Google Guaranteed badge ARE available in Belgium for plumbing
(support.google.com/localservices/answer/6224841, country BE): for the ads program (already a NOW item).
No-cure-no-pay is live in NL, absent in Belgium: available positioning but a real commercial commitment,
Roro's call, put as a question not a recommendation.
