# Credit note, phone talking points (2026-09-14)

For Fady, call with Roro in English, before the hand-over.

## 1. What it is
- A credit note is the only way to correct or cancel an invoice that is already sent.
- An invoice is never deleted and never rewritten.
- The credit note names the invoice it corrects, and a corrected invoice comes after it if one is needed.

## 2. When Steph uses it
- The amount on a sent invoice is too high (wrong price or wrong VAT rate).
- The customer was invoiced twice for the same job.
- The work was not done (job cancelled after the invoice went out).
- A mistake on the customer details makes the invoice unusable.
- Not for an invoice that has not gone out yet: she just corrects it. Not for a customer who does not pay: that is a reminder.

## 3. What is in the pack
- Note de credit FR, one page, for French customers.
- Creditnota NL, one page, for Dutch customers.
- The French guide, new section 9, tells her when to use it.
- She prints and fills them exactly like the invoice pack she already uses. Same look, same boxes.

## 4. The five rules for Steph
1. Own number series, NC-2026-001, NC-2026-002, in order, no gaps, never a number from the invoice series.
2. It always names the invoice it corrects: number, date and the reason.
3. The amounts are written positive. The paper says it takes them off the invoice.
4. The VAT sentence on it is printed and locked: never shorten it, never reword it.
5. Keep everything 10 years, like the invoices. A credit note on a business invoice sent by Peppol goes back out by Peppol.
