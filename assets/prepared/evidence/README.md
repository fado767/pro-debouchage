## 3. The verification video, shot order (faithful summary of `research/01` A4, about 75 seconds)

1. One unbroken take, no cuts, filmed LIVE inside the verification flow on a phone signed in as the owning account. Restart if interrupted.
2. The street, about 15 s: wide pan over the street name sign, the house number and a landmark. Most skipped shot and the most common single reason for rejection.
3. The vehicle as the business, about 20 s: plate, branded signage, a second angle.
4. Proof of control, about 10 s: take the keys out, unlock the van on camera, open the door, get in.
5. Tools of the trade, about 15 s: the equipment inside.
6. The paperwork, about 15 s, held flat and readable. Skip this shot entirely if the document name differs from the profile name (here it matches, so it stays).
7. Close on the person with the vehicle.
8. Pre-flight: van clean, anything with an old name out of shot, van LOCKED before the take, daylight, hold 3 to 4 seconds on anything with text, no customer faces, voice better left out, phone above 50 percent, Do Not Disturb ON, one dry run watched back.
