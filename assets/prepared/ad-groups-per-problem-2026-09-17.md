## 5. What this changes, and what it does not

This splits the two ad groups into eight and gives each problem its own page. A search for a blocked toilet now lands on a page whose first screen says "WC bouché", instead of the general landing page, which is what "landing page experience below average" was measuring: message match, not page quality.
