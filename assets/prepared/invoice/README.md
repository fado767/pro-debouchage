## The one command that rebuilds the pack

```
node assets/prepared/invoice/build-invoice.js
```

Run from the project root. It writes all five files: the two invoices, the two credit notes and the guide. Same input, byte-identical output, every run, so re-running it changes nothing that did not need changing. One file only, on purpose, with its target name: `node assets/prepared/invoice/build-invoice.js note-credit-fr` (targets: `facture-fr`, `factuur-nl`, `note-credit-fr`, `creditnota-nl`, `guide-fr`, plus `A-fr` for the rejected layout A).

## Which file to use

`PRO-DEBOUCHAGE-factuur-NL.docx` (Dutch) is the **default**. The company seat is in Vilvoorde, so it sits in the Flemish Region, and the Flemish language decree wants the papers of the employer relationship and the official documents in Dutch. Dutch is the safe one.

`PRO-DEBOUCHAGE-facture-FR.docx` (French) is for French-speaking customers, in Brussels and in Wallonia, and for any customer who asks for French. A French copy handed to a French-speaking customer next to the Dutch one is fine; the Dutch one is the one that always exists.

Simple rule for Steph: Dutch customer, Dutch file. French customer, French file. Doubt, send both.

## What is in each invoice file

1. **Page 1, FACTURE / FACTUUR.** The invoice template. Logo, full legal identity on one line (SRL and RPM Bruxelles in French, BV and RPR Brussel in Dutch, seat, VAT number, phone, e-mail, site), invoice number and dates (the label reads "N° de facture", the old "N° DE DEVIS" mistake is gone), an optional deposit line, the client block with the intervention address, six blank service lines with price excl. VAT per line, the VAT table (6 and 21 percent rows), the totals, the payment block with the IBAN and the late-payment rules, and the two legal paragraphs (the 6 percent attestation sentence and the reverse-charge sentence).
2. **Page 2, BON D'INTERVENTION / WERKBON.** The work order. **Verified present in both live files on 2026-08-30**: the filenames dropped the words, the page did not. It carries the company block, the client and intervention blocks, the urgent-works waiver, the immediate-execution consent, the announced price, the surcharge tickboxes (evening or Saturday +50 percent, night, Sunday or holiday +75 percent), the 60-euro no-fix line, the conditions acceptance with the 30-day guarantee and its exclusions, and the two signature blocks. The sheet itself now says it is **used when it is useful**, and when it is used it is signed BEFORE the work starts and the customer keeps a copy. Signed after the work, it is worthless: the customer can then legally cancel for 14 days after the job. Who carries it and who fills what is the open question in `playbook/invoicing-flow.md` section 2 (Claude's proposal: Afrim writes only the date and the price, the customer signs).
3. **Pages 3+, CONDITIONS GÉNÉRALES / ALGEMENE VOORWAARDEN.** The same conditions as on the website, print version, so the client really receives them.

## The layout

A small logo on the left, the word FACTURE / FACTUUR large on the right on the same line, one teal rule under them, and the whole legal identity as one quiet grey line across the page. Every place to write is a real content control with a soft grey fill and a thin rule, with the label in small type above it, tall enough for a pen in the van (the one exception is the optional deposit line, which is still written on rules). The totals table is the anchor at the bottom right of page 1, its last row in soft teal. The conditions pages are set as a proper terms sheet, article by article. Both languages come out of one script so they stay identical. Layout A, the tall centred letterhead, was the rejected candidate; its code path stays in `build-invoice.js` (target `A-fr`) so the choice can be revisited.
