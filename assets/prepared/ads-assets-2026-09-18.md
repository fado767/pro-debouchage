## 2. Two extra sitelinks per group, 4 to 6

Google's own recommendation card in this account asks for at least 6 sitelinks (research/34 section 13, "+2.7%"), and research/44 section 4 puts assets at the top of what can still move Ad Rank while the bid stays capped.

**One honest limit, found while writing this.** The site has exactly three section anchors per page, `#prix`, `#contenu` and `#zone`, plus the terms page. The camera promise, the 30 day guarantee, the anti scam rules and the who comes to your door block have NO anchors of their own, so they cannot be linked directly. The two emergency groups already use all three main page anchors plus the terms page, so a single pair of destinations shared by all eight groups is impossible: it would either repeat a destination the group already has or point a sitelink at the ad's own final URL, which shows the caller a page he is already on. So the pair below comes in two flavours, the SAME two jobs to do (who comes, and the guarantee, said as the problem groups need it), with destinations that are new for the group that gets them. A later site pass that adds `id="garantie"`, `id="camera"` and `id="qui"` to the page would let one pair serve all eight; that is a site change, not an ads change, and it is not made here.
