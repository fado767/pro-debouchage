# SCORE.md, the weekly numbers of this venture

*One line per week, appended by this folder's own session at its close (the Monday read, or the first close of the week). Newest at the bottom. Under 300 bytes a line. Only numbers that arrive without a person (the ad account's own counts: spend, calls from ads, booking-button clicks, orders) plus at most one human number marked "reported" (rides or jobs as the customer says them). A week with no reading gets the line "no number this week". The HQ reads the last line of this file for the Monday brief and nothing else in this folder (its one exception to the separation rule, Fady's yes of 2026-09-23). Nothing here is a task; the folder's open items live in its own files.*

Shape: `W<iso week> (<from> to <to>) | <the one number that matters> | spend <n> EUR | reported: <n> <rides or jobs> (whose word) | <one word on the checks: ok, or what failed>`

Example (invented): `W40 (2026-09-28 to 2026-10-04) | Mulu 52 booking clicks, 8.24 EUR each | spend 127 EUR | reported: 6 rides (Mulu, WhatsApp) | checks ok`
